对美洲安第斯山脉病毒传播的预测监测。

2026 年与 M/V Hondius 游轮有关的安第斯病毒爆发暴露了传统的基于病例的监测方法的局限性，以监测可能人传人的人畜共患病威胁。在目前的观点和分析文章中，作者认为，在人工智能、生态模型和“同一个健康”方法的支持下，预测性监测可以加强美洲对汉坦病毒传播的准备。目前的证据表明，纳入流行病学、气候、环境和啮齿动物相关变量的选定模型可能会提供 2 至 12 个月的预期窗口，具体取决于背景和数据输入。然而，实施需要的不仅仅是技术能力。它依赖于可互操作的多部门数据、透明的模型治理、当地验证、可持续融资和技术转让，以避免卫生系统之间不平等现象的扩大。预测性监测应通过支持风险沟通、啮齿动物监测、实验室准备以及在生态和流行病学信号汇聚的地区采取有针对性的公共卫生行动来补充传统监测。

Predictive Surveillance for Andes Virus Transmission in the Americas.

The 2026 Andes virus outbreak linked to the M/V Hondius cruise ship exposed limitations in conventional, case-based surveillance for zoonotic threats with potential person-to-person transmission. In the present opinion and analysis article, the authors argue that predictive surveillance, supported by artificial intelligence, ecological modeling, and the One Health approach, can strengthen preparedness for hantavirus transmission in the Americas. Current evidence suggests that selected models that incorporate epidemiological, climatic, environmental, and rodent-related variables may provide an anticipatory window of 2 to 12 months, depending on context and data inputs. However, implementation requires more than technical capacity. It depends on interoperable multisectoral data, transparent model governance, local validation, sustainable financing, and technology transfer to avoid widening inequities between health systems. Predictive surveillance should complement conventional surveillance by supporting risk communication, rodent monitoring, laboratory preparedness, and targeted public health action in areas where ecological and epidemiological signals converge.

美国西南部汉坦病毒毛细血管渗漏综合征病例报告。

汉坦病毒心肺综合征 (HCPS) 是一种罕见但经常致命的美洲流行病毒性疾病，主要通过吸入雾化的啮齿动物排泄物传播。早期症状是非特异性的，但由于严重的毛细血管渗漏，该疾病可能迅速发展为严重的呼吸系统损害和休克。我们报告了一名先前健康的 20 岁男子的病例，他在亚利桑那州西南部的一个牧场进行职业暴露后，最初出现腹痛、恶心、呕吐、腹泻和进行性无力。他很快出现血液浓缩（血细胞比容 58%）、血小板减少症（17,000 个细胞/μL）、感染性休克和非心源性肺水肿。最初的感染性检查结果呈阴性。他的临床病程因呼吸衰竭而变得复杂，需要插管和升压药支持。胸部计算机断层扫描显示，尽管积极利尿，但仍有肺水肿和大量双侧胸腔积液，需要双侧胸管。在支持治疗下，他的情况逐渐好转，成功拔管，戒掉氧气，并通过门诊康复出院回家。后来通过血清 ELISA 测试证实了他的诊断，结果显示汉坦病毒 IgM 和 IgG 呈阳性。该病例凸显了 HCPS 诊断的复杂性，特别是当胃肠道症状在疾病早期占主导地位时。在有啮齿动物接触史的流行地区，血液浓缩和血小板减少，以及容量超负荷和快速进行性急性呼吸衰竭的迹象，应怀疑汉坦病毒感染。识别和支持性护理是治疗的支柱。尽管该患者不需要 ECMO，但根据疾病控制中心的建议，转移到 ECMO 中心可能有利于支持心肺期的康复。

A Case Report of Hantavirus Capillary Leak Syndrome in the Southwestern United States.

Hantavirus cardiopulmonary syndrome (HCPS) is a rare but frequently fatal viral disease endemic to the Americas, transmitted primarily through inhalation of aerosolized rodent excreta. Early symptoms are nonspecific, but the disease can rapidly progress to severe respiratory compromise and shock due to profound capillary leak. We report the case of a previously healthy 20-year-old man who initially presented with abdominal pain, nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, and progressive weakness after occupational exposure on a ranch in southwestern Arizona. He quickly developed hemoconcentration (hematocrit 58%), thrombocytopenia (17,000 cells/μL), septic shock, and noncardiogenic pulmonary edema. The initial infectious workup was negative. His clinical course was complicated by respiratory failure requiring intubation and vasopressor support. Computed tomography of his chest showed pulmonary edema and large bilateral pleural effusions despite aggressive diuresis, requiring bilateral chest tubes. He gradually improved with supportive care, was successfully extubated, weaned off oxygen, and discharged home with outpatient rehabilitation. His diagnosis was later confirmed with a serum ELISA test which was positive for hantavirus IgM and IgG. This case highlights the diagnostic complexity of HCPS, particularly when gastrointestinal symptoms predominate early in the illness. In endemic areas with rodent exposure history, hemoconcentration and thrombocytopenia, along with signs of volume overload and rapidly progressive acute respiratory failure, should raise suspicion for hantavirus infection. Identification and supportive care are the mainstay of treatment. Although this patient did not require ECMO, transfer to an ECMO center may be beneficial to support recovery during the cardiopulmonary phase, as recommended by the Centers for Disease Control.

安第斯病毒疫苗衍生的人抗体对叙利亚仓鼠的保护作用评估：使用一期试验血清的被动转移研究。

安第斯病毒 (ANDV) 会导致严重的汉坦病毒肺部综合征，并且具有独特的人际传播能力，目前尚无获得许可的疫苗或治疗方法。一期试验 (NCT03682107) 表明，针对 Gn/Gc 包膜蛋白的 ANDV DNA 疫苗可在人体中引发中和抗体。我们评估了这些人类抗体是否可以在动物模型中预防致命疾病。在两次独立实验中，在致死性 ANDV 攻击前一天，将来自已接种疫苗的人类受试者的第 197 天血清汇集、纯化并被动转移至雌性叙利亚仓鼠。对照包括正常人 IgG、PBS（阴性对照）和 SAB-400（阳性对照）。使用伪病毒粒子中和试验（PsVNA）和噬菌斑减少中和试验（PRNT）对中和抗体水平进行定量，以确定预测存活的探索性截止滴度。与正常 IgG 不同，来自 DNA 疫苗接种受试者的人类抗体在两项实验中都提供了针对致命性 ANDV 攻击的显着保护作用。根据分析群体和模型参数，预测保护作用的探索性截止滴度确定为 PsVNA50 的 31-184 和 PRNT50 的 20-80。在已建立的动物模型中，疫苗诱导的人类抗体足以防止致命的 ANDV 感染。如果得到证实，这些结果表明中和抗体滴度可以作为替代终点并与未来汉坦病毒 Gn/Gc 靶向疫苗的保护相关。国防健康计划和美国国立卫生研究院 (NIH)。

Evaluation of Andes virus vaccine-derived human antibody for protection in Syrian hamsters: a passive transfer study using phase 1 trial sera.

Andes virus (ANDV) causes severe hantavirus pulmonary syndrome and is uniquely capable of person-to-person transmission, with no licenced vaccines or therapeutics available. A Phase 1 trial (NCT03682107) demonstrated that an ANDV DNA vaccine targeting Gn/Gc envelope proteins elicits neutralising antibodies in humans. We evaluated whether these human antibodies protect against lethal disease in an animal model. Day 197 sera from vaccinated human subjects were pooled, purified, and passively transferred to female Syrian hamsters one day prior to lethal ANDV challenge across two independent experiments. Controls included normal human IgG, PBS (negative controls), and SAB-400 (positive control). Neutralising antibody levels were quantified using pseudovirion neutralisation assays (PsVNA) and plaque reduction neutralisation tests (PRNT) to determine exploratory cutoff titres predicting survival. Human antibodies from DNA-vaccinated subjects-unlike normal IgG-conferred significant protection against lethal ANDV challenge in both experiments. The exploratory cutoff titres predicting protection were determined to be 31-184 for PsVNA50 and 20-80 for PRNT50, depending on the analysis population and model parameters. Vaccine-induced human antibodies are sufficient to protect against lethal ANDV infection in an established animal model. If confirmed, these results indicate that neutralising antibody titres could serve as a surrogate endpoint and correlate of protection for future hantavirus Gn/Gc-targeted vaccines. Defense Health Program and the National Institutes of Health (NIH).

综合免疫信息学和分子动力学方法揭示了一种有前途的针对汉坦病毒的多表位疫苗。

汉坦病毒是一种啮齿类动物传播的人畜共患病原体，可引起肾综合征出血热 (HFRS) 和汉坦病毒肺综合征 (HPS)，在全球范围内具有很高的发病率和死亡率。目前还没有得到普遍批准的人类疫苗，因此安全、有效的疫苗成为公共卫生优先事项。采用免疫信息学策略设计针对汉坦正汉坦病毒糖蛋白前体（GP_HANTL）的多表位疫苗构建体（MEVC）。预测并筛选细胞毒性 T 淋巴细胞 (CTL)、辅助 T 淋巴细胞 (HTL) 和 B 细胞表位的抗原性、免疫原性、MHC 结合亲和力和群体覆盖率。选定的表位与 EAAAK、AAY、GPPGG 和 KK 连接体连接，并与 β-防御素佐剂融合。通过物理化学表征、结构建模、分子对接、分子动力学（MD）模拟、免疫模拟、密码子优化和计算机克隆对构建体进行评估。最终构建体包含 10 个 CTL、10 个 HTL 和 5 个 B 细胞表位，全球人群覆盖率达到 99.24%。结构验证显示 95% 的残基位于有利的 Ramachandran 区域，ERRAT 评分为 82。分子对接为选定的 MEVC-TLR4 复合物产生了有利的 ClusPro 评分 (- 1273.6)。免疫模拟预测了强烈的体液和细胞反应，包括抗体升高、记忆 B 细胞和 CTL/HTL 激活。密码子优化产生的 CAI 为 0.94，支持大肠杆菌的高效表达。 100 ns MD 模拟显示 MEVC 的显着构象适应，而 TLR4 保持相对稳定；伴随的 Rg 和 SASA 趋势表明 MEVC 在轨迹过程中采用了更紧凑的状态。所提出的 MEVC 表现出良好的预测免疫原性、良好的结构特征、广泛的人群覆盖度和良好的表达特征。这些发现支持实验验证和制定针对汉坦病毒感染的预防策略。在线版本包含 https://doi.org/10.1007/s40203-026-00782-6 上提供的补充材料。

Integrated immunoinformatics and molecular dynamics approaches reveal a promising multi-epitope vaccine against hantavirus.

Hantavirus is a rodent-borne zoonotic pathogen causing Hemorrhagic Fever with Renal Syndrome (HFRS) and Hantavirus Pulmonary Syndrome (HPS), with substantial global morbidity and mortality. No universally approved human vaccine exists, making a safe, effective vaccine a public health priority. An immunoinformatics strategy was used to design a multi-epitope vaccine construct (MEVC) targeting the glycoprotein precursor (GP_HANTL) of Hantaan orthohantavirus. Cytotoxic T lymphocyte (CTL), helper T lymphocyte (HTL), and B-cell epitopes were predicted and screened for antigenicity, immunogenicity, MHC-binding affinity, and population coverage. Selected epitopes were joined with EAAAK, AAY, GPGPG, and KK linkers and fused to a β-defensin adjuvant. The construct was evaluated by physicochemical characterization, structural modeling, molecular docking, molecular dynamics (MD) simulation, immune simulation, codon optimization, and in silico cloning. The final construct comprised 10 CTL, 10 HTL, and 5 B-cell epitopes, with 99.24% global population coverage. Structural validation showed 95% of residues in favored Ramachandran regions and an ERRAT score of 82. Molecular docking produced a favorable ClusPro score (- 1273.6) for the selected MEVC-TLR4 complex. Immune simulations predicted robust humoral and cellular responses, including elevated antibodies, memory B cells, and CTL/HTL activation. Codon optimization yielded a CAI of 0.94, supporting efficient Escherichia coli expression. A 100-ns MD simulation showed substantial conformational adaptation of the MEVC while TLR4 remained comparatively stable; accompanying Rg and SASA trends indicated that the MEVC adopted a more compact state during the trajectory. The proposed MEVC demonstrated favorable predicted immunogenicity, favorable structural characteristics, broad population coverage, and favorable expression characteristics. These findings support experimental validation and development of a preventive strategy against Hantavirus infection. Hantavirus on a Cruise Ship: What COVID-19 Taught Us.

Delayed recognition of the mechanics of aerosol transmission-the primary mode of spread of severe acute respiratory syndrome coronavirus 2 (SARS-CoV-2)-at the beginning of the coronavirus disease 2019 (COVID-19) pandemic led to inadequate early recommendations regarding nonpharmaceutical interventions and poor initial messaging about how to reduce infection risk. We highlight key epidemiological details of Andes virus (ANDV) transmission on the MV Hondius cruise ship and during prior reported outbreaks with confirmed person-to-person spread in Argentina, which, together, suggest aerosol spread may be a key mechanism of transmission of ANDV between source cases and susceptible contacts in permissible environmental settings.
