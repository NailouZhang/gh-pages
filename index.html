PV-GNProtecta 的设计和计算机评估：使用免疫信息学和结构分析，针对 Puumala 病毒的 mRNA 多表位候选疫苗。

普马拉病毒 (PUUV) 是流行性肾病 (NE) 的主要病原体，由于其人畜共患传播、肾脏并发症和缺乏批准的疫苗，对公共卫生构成重大威胁。在这项研究中，利用病毒糖蛋白 (G) 和核蛋白 (N) 的保守区域设计了一种免疫信息学驱动的 mRNA 多表位疫苗 PV-GNProtecta。鉴定出高抗原性、非过敏性和无毒的 T 细胞和 B 细胞表位，并与适当的佐剂组装以增强免疫原性。人群覆盖率分析表明不同人群之间存在广泛的 HLA 反应性。该疫苗构建体表现出良好的理化特性，包括稳定性、溶解度和抗原性。结构验证预测模型质量较高，95.5% 的残基位于有利的 Ramachandran 区域，ERRAT 得分为 91.4。分子对接和 MM-GBSA 分析预测了 PV-GNProtecta 和 TLR4 之间有利的计算相互作用，而 100 ns 分子动力学模拟提供了对疫苗-TLR4 复合物动态行为的计算见解。密码子优化表明设计的序列与基于大肠杆菌的表达框架兼容。免疫模拟预测了潜在的体液和细胞免疫反应模式，包括 IgG、IFN-γ 和 IL-2 水平的变化。设计的mRNA序列表现出良好的预测二级结构特征；然而，需要进一步的实验评估来评估翻译效率和稳定性。尽管这项研究仅限于计算分析并需要实验验证，但研究结果表明 PV-GNProtecta 可能代表进一步研究 PUUV 感染的潜在候选者。

Design and in silico evaluation of PV-GNProtecta: an mRNA multi-epitope vaccine candidate against Puumala virus using immunoinformatics and structural analysis.

Puumala virus (PUUV), the principal causative agent of nephropathia epidemica (NE), poses a significant public health threat due to its zoonotic transmission, renal complications, and the lack of an approved vaccine. In this study, an immunoinformatics-driven mRNA multi-epitope vaccine, PV-GNProtecta, was designed using conserved regions of the viral glycoprotein (G) and nucleoprotein (N). Highly antigenic, non-allergenic, and non-toxic T-cell and B-cell epitopes were identified and assembled with appropriate adjuvants to enhance immunogenicity. Population coverage analysis suggested broad HLA responsiveness across diverse populations. The vaccine construct exhibited favorable physicochemical properties, including stability, solubility, and antigenicity. Structural validation predicted high model quality, with 95.5% of residues located in favored Ramachandran regions and an ERRAT score of 91.4. Molecular docking and MM-GBSA analyses predicted favorable computational interactions between PV-GNProtecta and TLR4, while a 100 ns molecular dynamics simulation provided computational insights into the dynamic behavior of the vaccine-TLR4 complex. Codon optimization suggested compatibility of the designed sequence with an Escherichia coli-based expression framework. Immune simulation predicted potential humoral and cellular immune response patterns, including changes in IgG, IFN-γ, and IL-2 levels. The designed mRNA sequence exhibited favorable predicted secondary structural characteristics; however, further experimental evaluation is required to assess translational efficiency and stability. Although this study is limited to computational analyses and requires experimental validation, the findings suggest that PV-GNProtecta may represent a potential candidate for further investigation against PUUV infection.
