人类汉坦病毒病血清学、分子和快速检测的诊断准确性：系统评价和荟萃分析。

人类汉坦病毒病具有潜在致命性，并且在临床上与其他发热、肾脏和心肺综合征有重叠。诊断性能因病毒种类、标本类型、采样时间和参考标准而异。综合并比较人类汉坦病毒感染或疾病的血清学、分子、快速、多重和抗原检测检测的诊断准确性。我们进行了在 PROSPERO (CRD420261398030) 中注册的系统评价和诊断测试准确性荟萃分析。 MEDLINE/PubMed、Embase、Scopus 和 Web of Science 的检索截止日期为 2026 年 5 月 20 日。包含具有合格索引测试和可验证参考标准的人体研究。尽可能提取诊断准确性数据。使用 QUADAS-2 评估偏倚风险，使用 GRADE-DTA 评估确定性。使用特定于平台的单变量 Logit 尺度模型进行初步荟萃分析；对符合条件的分层进行了分层双变量/HSROC 敏感性分析。纳入了 63 项研究； 24个贡献了2×2数据，22个初步比较进入了主综合。 ELISA/EIA 的敏感性为 98.0%，特异性为 98.8%，但确定性较低。快速检测的灵敏度为 97.4%，特异性为 97.2%，但确定性也较低。分子检测的特异性为99.5%，敏感性为85.6%。 ELISA/EIA 和快速格式测定的双变量敏感性分析产生了与主要模型相似的估计值。在现有的低质量证据中，ELISA/EIA 血清学是支持最好的平台，但这并不是普遍建议。分子和快速测试在特定情况下可能有用，并且需要前瞻性验证。

Diagnostic accuracy of serological, molecular and rapid tests for human hantavirus disease: a systematic review and meta-analysis.

Human hantavirus disease is potentially lethal and overlaps clinically with other febrile, renal, and cardiopulmonary syndromes. Diagnostic performance varies by viral species, specimen type, sampling timing, and reference standard. To synthesize and compare the diagnostic accuracy of serologic, molecular, rapid, multiplex, and antigen-detection tests for human hantavirus infection or disease. We conducted a systematic review and diagnostic test accuracy meta-analysis registered in PROSPERO (CRD420261398030). MEDLINE/PubMed, Embase, Scopus, and Web of Science were searched through May 20, 2026. Human studies with an eligible index test and verifiable reference standard were included. Diagnostic accuracy data were extracted when possible. Risk of bias was assessed with QUADAS-2 and certainty with GRADE-DTA. Primary meta-analyses used platform-specific univariate logit-scale models; hierarchical bivariate/HSROC sensitivity analyses were performed for eligible strata. Sixty-three studies were included; 24 contributed 2 × 2 data and 22 primary comparisons entered the main synthesis. ELISA/EIA showed sensitivity of 98.0% and specificity of 98.8%, with low certainty. Rapid-format assays showed sensitivity of 97.4% and specificity of 97.2%, also with low certainty. Molecular tests had specificity of 99.5% and sensitivity of 85.6%. Bivariate sensitivity analyses for ELISA/EIA and rapid-format assays produced similar estimates to the primary models. In the available low-certainty evidence, ELISA/EIA serology is the best-supported platform, but this is not a universal recommendation. Molecular and rapid tests may be useful in specific contexts and require prospective validation.

CD8+ 调节性 T 细胞限制免疫病理学，但损害汉坦病毒感染中的病毒清除。

在汉坦病毒 (HTNV) 引起的肾综合征出血热 (HFRS) 中，病毒特异性 CD8+ T 细胞介导病毒清除和免疫病理学。在这里，我们在 HFRS 患者和小鼠 HTNV 感染模型中描述了受 HLA-E（及其小鼠直系同源物 Qa-1）限制的 CD8+ 调节性 T 细胞 (Treg) 亚群。 CD8+ Tregs (CD122+Eomes+CD49dloKIR+) 是 HLA-E 限制性 T 细胞显着富集的子集，在 HFRS 患者外周血中显着减少，并表现出终末分化特征，其中 KLRG-1、NKG2A、CTLA-4 和 Fas-L 表达升高，与疾病严重程度相关。鉴定出两种结合 Qa-1 的 HTNV 糖蛋白衍生九肽。使用已鉴定的 HTNV 肽进行免疫并随后进行 HTNV 攻击后，野生型 (WT) 小鼠表现出肾脏免疫病理学减弱，而 D227K 敲入小鼠（Qa-1-CD8/TCR 相互作用被破坏）表现出脾脏和肾脏中病毒清除率增强，但肾脏免疫病理学加剧。从机制上讲，WT 小鼠的 Qa-1 限制性 CD8+ Tregs 通过增强 TGF-β 分泌，以肽特异性、CD8/TCR 依赖性方式抑制效应 CD8+ T 细胞增殖。引人注目的是，D227K 突变以二分法重塑了 Treg 表型：大量多克隆 CD8+ Tregs 显示出抑制性受体（NKG2A、CTLA-4、PD-1）和 TGF-β 的代偿性上调，而表位特异性 CD8+ Tregs 显示出抗原特异性抑制功能受损并减弱了 TGF-β 的产生。总的来说，这些发现表明 HLA-E/Qa-1 限制性 CD8+ Tregs 可以抑制 HTNV 感染期间的免疫病理学，可能会以延迟病毒消除为代价。该轴的调节可能为 HFRS 和相关病毒性出血热的治疗策略提供见解。

CD8+ regulatory T cells limit immunopathology but impair viral clearance in Hantaan virus infection.

In hemorrhagic fever with renal syndrome (HFRS) caused by Hantaan virus (HTNV), virus-specific CD8+ T cells mediate both viral clearance and immunopathology. Here, we characterized a CD8+ regulatory T cell (Treg) subset restricted by HLA-E (and its murine ortholog Qa-1) in HFRS patients and in a murine HTNV infection model. CD8+ Tregs (CD122+Eomes+CD49dloKIR+), a subset substantially enriched for HLA-E-restricted T cells, were significantly reduced in peripheral blood of HFRS patients and displayed a terminally differentiated profile with elevated expression of KLRG-1, NKG2A, CTLA-4, and Fas-L, correlating with disease severity. Two HTNV glycoprotein-derived nonapeptides that bound Qa-1 were identified. Following immunization with the identified HTNV peptides and subsequent HTNV challenge, wild-type (WT) mice showed attenuated renal immunopathology, whereas D227K knock-in mice (with disrupted Qa-1-CD8/TCR interaction) showed enhanced viral clearance in the spleen and kidney but exacerbated renal immunopathology. Mechanistically, Qa-1-restricted CD8+ Tregs from WT mice suppressed effector CD8+ T cell proliferation in a peptide-specific, CD8/TCR-dependent manner via enhanced TGF-β secretion. Strikingly, D227K mutation dichotomously reshaped Treg phenotype: bulk polyclonal CD8+ Tregs displayed compensatory upregulation of inhibitory receptors (NKG2A, CTLA-4, PD-1) and TGF-β, whereas epitope-specific CD8+ Tregs showed impaired antigen-specific suppressive function and attenuated TGF-β production. Collectively, these findings indicate that HLA-E/Qa-1-restricted CD8+ Tregs can restrain immunopathology during HTNV infection, potentially at the cost of delayed viral elimination. Modulation of this axis may provide insights for therapeutic strategies in HFRS and related viral hemorrhagic fevers.

Predictive Surveillance for Andes Virus Transmission in the Americas.

The 2026 Andes virus outbreak linked to the M/V Hondius cruise ship exposed limitations in conventional, case-based surveillance for zoonotic threats with potential person-to-person transmission. In the present opinion and analysis article, the authors argue that predictive surveillance, supported by artificial intelligence, ecological modeling, and the One Health approach, can strengthen preparedness for hantavirus transmission in the Americas. Current evidence suggests that selected models that incorporate epidemiological, climatic, environmental, and rodent-related variables may provide an anticipatory window of 2 to 12 months, depending on context and data inputs. However, implementation requires more than technical capacity. It depends on interoperable multisectoral data, transparent model governance, local validation, sustainable financing, and technology transfer to avoid widening inequities between health systems. Predictive surveillance should complement conventional surveillance by supporting risk communication, rodent monitoring, laboratory preparedness, and targeted public health action in areas where ecological and epidemiological signals converge.

美国西南部汉坦病毒毛细血管渗漏综合征病例报告。

A Case Report of Hantavirus Capillary Leak Syndrome in the Southwestern United States.

Hantavirus cardiopulmonary syndrome (HCPS) is a rare but frequently fatal viral disease endemic to the Americas, transmitted primarily through inhalation of aerosolized rodent excreta. Early symptoms are nonspecific, but the disease can rapidly progress to severe respiratory compromise and shock due to profound capillary leak. We report the case of a previously healthy 20-year-old man who initially presented with abdominal pain, nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, and progressive weakness after occupational exposure on a ranch in southwestern Arizona. He quickly developed hemoconcentration (hematocrit 58%), thrombocytopenia (17,000 cells/μL), septic shock, and noncardiogenic pulmonary edema. The initial infectious workup was negative. His clinical course was complicated by respiratory failure requiring intubation and vasopressor support. Computed tomography of his chest showed pulmonary edema and large bilateral pleural effusions despite aggressive diuresis, requiring bilateral chest tubes. He gradually improved with supportive care, was successfully extubated, weaned off oxygen, and discharged home with outpatient rehabilitation. His diagnosis was later confirmed with a serum ELISA test which was positive for hantavirus IgM and IgG. This case highlights the diagnostic complexity of HCPS, particularly when gastrointestinal symptoms predominate early in the illness. In endemic areas with rodent exposure history, hemoconcentration and thrombocytopenia, along with signs of volume overload and rapidly progressive acute respiratory failure, should raise suspicion for hantavirus infection. Identification and supportive care are the mainstay of treatment. Although this patient did not require ECMO, transfer to an ECMO center may be beneficial to support recovery during the cardiopulmonary phase, as recommended by the Centers for Disease Control.
