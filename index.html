[Preparedness for summer illnesses and emergencies in Chile, 2024-2025: lessons from an innovative policyPreparação para doenças e emergências no verão de 2024-2025 no Chile: lições de uma política inovadora].

Climate change and the increasing frequency of extreme heat events have heightened the importance of health issues during the summer season. This season is also associated with greater population mobility, outdoor recreational activities, and exposure to biological and environmental risks, all of which require coordinated responses from health systems. Within this context, the Ministry of Health of Chile implemented the summer seasonal illness and emergency preparedness plan 2024-2025, with the goal of strengthening surveillance, intersectoral coordination, and risk communication in response to priority threats to population health.This article describes the design, implementation, and key results of this first national initiative to prepare for diseases occurring during the summer season. The strategy was based on a multilevel governance model that liaised between the central, regional, and local levels of the health system through national coordination meetings, integrated surveillance, strengthening of technical capacities, and public communications. During the season covered by the plan, detection of Aedes aegypti remained confined to areas where it was previously known to occur; 395 outbreaks of foodborne illnesses affecting 2618 people were investigated; 8 cases of hantavirus were reported, with no deaths; and environmental risks and emergencies were monitored, including 4330 forest fires, none of which affected health facilities.This experience demonstrates the feasibility of implementing integrated disease preparedness strategies during the summer season and provides lessons for their consolidation as public policy, including the formalization of coordination mechanisms, the systematic assessment of implementation, and the incorporation of new hazards, such as drowning and injuries associated with recreational water activities. La estrategia se sustentó en un modelo de gobernanza multinivel que articuló los niveles central, regional y local del sistema de salud, mediante sesiones nacionales de coordinación, vigilancia integrada, fortalecimiento de capacidades técnicas y vocerías públicas. 新发呼吸道传染病：快速识别和管理的临床方面。

新兴呼吸道传染病越来越多地出现在人类与动物环境的交界处，并给临床医生快速发展的诊断、治疗和感染控制决策带来挑战。这篇叙述性综述总结了临床相关的病毒和细菌病原体，这些病原体可能表现为严重的呼吸道疾病，但在疾病早期很容易被误认为是常见的社区获得性感染。我们专注于人畜共患甲型流感病毒，包括当代 H5N1、中东呼吸综合征冠状病毒 (MERS-CoV)、汉坦病毒、尼帕病毒，以及炭疽、肺鼠疫、兔热病和类鼻疽等吸入性细菌威胁。这些感染可能从非特异性症状发展为严重肺炎、急性呼吸窘迫综合征、休克、神经系统并发症、播散性感染或死亡。及时识别取决于对旅行、职业和娱乐暴露、动物接触、疫情信号和病原体特异性症状线索的仔细评估。多重分子诊断可以支持常见病原体的早期分类和排除，但靶向 PCR、培养、血清学、测序和参考实验室确认仍然至关重要。管理需要及时的支持性护理、适当的隔离、公共卫生通知以及针对病原体的抗菌、抗病毒、抗毒素或预防策略（如果有）。通过整合临床表现、诊断、治疗、疫苗和 One Health 考虑因素，本综述旨在将新发感染的知识转化为实际的床边准备。

Emerging respiratory infectious diseases: Clinical aspects for rapid identification and management.

Emerging respiratory infectious diseases increasingly arise at the human-animal-environment interface and challenge clinicians with rapidly evolving diagnostic, therapeutic, and infection-control decisions. This narrative review summarizes clinically relevant viral and bacterial pathogens that may present as severe respiratory disease, but are easily mistaken for common community-acquired infections during early illness. We focus on zoonotic influenza A viruses, including contemporary H5N1, Middle East respiratory syndrome coronavirus (MERS-CoV), hantaviruses, Nipah virus, and inhalational bacterial threats such as anthrax, pneumonic plague, tularemia, and melioidosis. These infections may progress from non-specific symptoms to severe pneumonia, acute respiratory distress syndrome, shock, neurological complications, disseminated infection, or death. Timely recognition depends on careful assessment of travel, occupational and recreational exposures, animal contact, outbreak signals, and pathogen-specific syndromic clues. Multiplex molecular diagnostics can support early triage and exclusion of common pathogens, but targeted PCR, culture, serology, sequencing, and reference laboratory confirmation remain essential. Management requires prompt supportive care, appropriate isolation, public health notification, and pathogen-directed antimicrobial, antiviral, antitoxin, or prophylactic strategies where available. By integrating clinical presentation, diagnostics, treatment, vaccines, and One Health considerations, this review aims to translate knowledge on emerging infections into practical bedside preparedness.

汉坦病毒心肺综合征：一种新出现的致命人畜共患感染。

汉坦病毒心肺综合征是由新大陆汉坦病毒引起的一种潜在致命的呼吸道人畜共患感染，主要通过吸入受感染啮齿动物的雾化排泄物而获得。在过去的 30 年里，HCPS 已经超出了最初的焦点，最近的事件表明有可能蔓延到传统流行地区之外。尽管采取了最佳的支持治疗，该疾病仍会从模糊的前驱症状迅速发展为急性心肺衰竭，死亡率很高。病理生理学涉及内皮衰竭、血管通透性增加、急性血小板减少症和导致毛细血管渗漏综合征和心源性休克的过度免疫反应。由于前驱症状模糊且反映了常见的病毒感染和呼吸系统疾病，因此临床上仍然很难及早怀疑。诊断确认需要血清学检测或遗传学方法，这在资源有限的流行情况下通常无法实现，从而导致相当大的诊断延误。管理通常是支持性的，包括早期入住 ICU、明智的水化管理、机械呼吸和心肺支持，包括难治性患者的体外膜肺氧合。最近 2026 年 5 月在一艘游轮上发生的多国安第斯山脉病毒疫情凸显了 HCPS 在通常的流行环境之外在全球范围内快速传播的可能性。本综述总结了有关 HCPS 流行病学、病理生理学、临床表现、诊断技术和治疗方案的最新信息。我们还强调，迫切需要改善全球监测、快速即时诊断、提高医护人员的全球意识，以及不断开发预防性疫苗和有效的抗病毒疗法，以应对这一重大的公共卫生威胁。

Hantavirus cardiopulmonary syndrome: an emerging fatal zoonotic infection.

Hantavirus cardiopulmonary syndrome is a potentially fatal respiratory zoonotic infection caused by New World hantaviruses, and it is acquired mostly via inhaling aerosolized excreta from infected rodents. Over the past 30 years, HCPS has expanded beyond its initial foci, and recent events suggest a potential for spread beyond traditionally endemic regions. The disease rapidly progresses from a vague prodrome to acute cardiopulmonary failure with a high fatality rate despite optimum supportive treatment. Pathophysiology involves endothelial failure, increased vascular permeability, acute thrombocytopenia and excessive immunological responses resulting in capillary leak syndrome and cardiogenic shock. It is still hard to clinically suspect early since the prodromal symptoms are vague and mirror common viral infections and respiratory disorders. Diagnostic confirmation needs serological testing or genetic approaches, which are typically unavailable in resource-limited endemic situations, leading to considerable diagnostic delays. Management is generally supportive, with early ICU admission, judicious hydration management, mechanical breathing and cardiopulmonary support including extracorporeal membrane oxygenation in refractory patients. The recent multi-country Andes virus epidemic on a cruise ship in May 2026 highlights the potential for fast worldwide transmission of HCPS outside of usual endemic settings. This review summarizes the current information on the epidemiology, pathophysiology, clinical presentation, diagnostic techniques, and treatment options for HCPS. We also highlight the pressing need for improved global surveillance, rapid point-of-care diagnostics, global awareness among health care workers, and ongoing development of preventive vaccines and effective antiviral therapies to address this significant public health threat.

驱动磁性 3D DNA 步行器与基于光激活发夹的 CHA 相结合：超灵敏汉坦病毒 RNA 传感的双扩增策略。

大别山正汉坦病毒（DBSV）是一种新出现的汉坦病毒，对人类健康构成潜在威胁。目前基于聚合酶链反应（PCR）检测其他汉坦病毒（例如汉坦病毒（HTNV）和首尔病毒（SEOV））的检测方法可能存在假阴性的风险。本研究旨在解决目前DBSV检测准确度低、灵敏度不足的技术瓶颈。我们开发了一种由光激活磁性 3D DNA 步行器介导的信号放大策略，可实现光控荧光检测。具体来说，该荧光传感平台引入了用于双信号放大的催化发夹组装（CHA）技术。该系统需要紫外线照射和靶标RNA双重激活。然后，核酸外切酶 III (Exo III) 为行走过程提供推进力，释放特定的触发链，随后激活 CHA 循环并产生荧光信号。实验结果证实，该系统表现出优异的选择性和抗干扰能力，成功区分出单碱基错配（SBM）、双碱基错配（DBM）和三碱基错配（TBM）的目标序列。此外，该平台的 DBSV-RNA 检测限 (LOD) 为 30fM，线性范围涵盖 10-1-106 pM。该方法的适用性已使用人血清样本进行了严格验证。这项研究为时空可控DNA步行者在RNA检测中的应用提供了一种新策略，展示了早期病毒诊断和现场监测的巨大潜力。

Driven magnetic 3D DNA walker coupled with CHA based on light-activatable hairpin: A dual-amplification strategy for ultrasensitive hantavirus RNA sensing.

Orthohantavirus dabieshanense (DBSV) is a newly emerging hantavirus and poses a potential threat to human health. Current detection methods based on polymerase chain reaction (PCR) assays for other hantaviruses such as Hantaan virus (HTNV) and Seoul virus (SEOV) may carry the risk of false negatives. This study aims to address the technical bottlenecks of low accuracy and insufficient sensitivity in current detection of DBSV. We developed a signal amplification strategy mediated by a photoactivated magnetic 3D DNA walker, which enables light-controllable fluorescence detection. Specifically, this fluorescence sensing platform introduces the catalytic hairpin assembly (CHA) technique for dual signal amplification. This system requires dual activation by UV irradiation and target RNA. Then, exonuclease III (Exo III) provides propulsion for the walking process to release a specific trigger strand, subsequently activating the CHA cycle and producing a fluorescent signal. Experimental results confirm that the system displays excellent selectivity and anti-interference ability, successfully distinguishing target sequences with single-base mismatch (SBM), double-base mismatch (DBM), and triple-base mismatch (TBM). Furthermore, the platform achieves a DBSV-RNA detection limit (LOD) of 30 fM with a linear range spanning 10-1-106 pM. The applicability of the method has been rigorously validated using human serum specimens.
