持续性白细胞减少症掩盖了肾综合征出血热。

肾综合征出血热 (HFRS) 通常伴有发烧、面部潮红、皮疹、血小板减少、急性肾损伤、凝血功能障碍和白细胞增多。然而，不典型的表现可能会掩盖诊断。一名接触啮齿动物的 30 岁男性出现高烧、血小板减少和持续性白细胞减少，尽管有轻度蛋白尿形式的肾脏受累，但没有明显的肾功能障碍。通过血清学和RT-PCR证实汉滩病毒。患者在利巴韦林和支持治疗后康复。该病例强调，在 HFRS 流行地区，即使没有明显的肾功能不全，出现不明原因发热、血小板减少和持续性白细胞减少而不是典型白细胞增多的患者也应怀疑汉坦病毒感染。

Persistent leukopenia masks hemorrhagic fever with renal syndrome.

Hemorrhagic fever with renal syndrome (HFRS) typically comes with fever, facial flushing, rash, thrombocytopenia, acute kidney injury, coagulopathy and leukocytosis. However, atypical manifestations may obscure diagnosis. A 30-year-old man with rodent exposure had high fever, thrombocytopenia, and persistent leukopenia, without overt renal dysfunction despite mild renal involvement in the form of proteinuria. Hantaan virus was confirmed by serology and RT-PCR. The patient recovered following ribavirin and supportive management. This case highlights that in HFRS-endemic regions, hantavirus infection should be suspected among patients with unexplained fever, thrombocytopenia, and persistent leukopenia rather than the typical leukocytosis, even without overt renal dysfunction.

汉坦病毒感染和自身免疫的界限：血管免疫病理学、自身免疫样拟态和感染后现象。

正汉坦病毒是啮齿类动物传播的RNA病毒，可引起急性全身性血管综合征，主要是肾综合征出血热和汉坦病毒心肺综合征。虽然主要是传染性的，但汉坦病毒病可能会产生与免疫介导疾病重叠的血管、肾脏、血液和全身表型。本综述严格审查了感染驱动的免疫病理学和自身免疫之间的界限。汉坦病毒感染涉及内皮功能障碍、先天抗病毒感应、干扰素反应、细胞因子和趋化因子放大、补体激活、免疫血栓形成和适应性免疫反应，其表达进一步受到病毒、宿主和组织特异性因素的影响。这些机制导致临床疾病的显着异质性，并可能重现自身免疫样临床和实验室特征，但其本身并不造成免疫耐受的持续丧失。目前对于急性感染相关的免疫病理学和表型拟态的证据比对于持续性自身反应性或因果性感染后自身免疫性疾病的证据要强得多。报道的自身免疫现象仍然不常见，并且主要基于病例，而纵向、抗原特异性和流行病学证据有限。因此，感染消退后持续或反复出现的表现需要额外的血清学、组织病理学或持续免疫失调的其他客观证据。因此，汉坦病毒感染提供了一个临床信息模型，用于区分抗病毒免疫病理学与自身免疫样拟态和罕见的可能的感染后自身免疫，对诊断和治疗限制具有直接影响。带回家的信息。

Hantavirus infection and the boundaries of autoimmunity: Vascular immunopathology, autoimmune-like mimicry, and post-infectious phenomena.

Orthohantaviruses are rodent-borne RNA viruses causing acute systemic vascular syndromes, principally haemorrhagic fever with renal syndrome and hantavirus cardiopulmonary syndrome. Although primarily infectious, hantavirus disease may generate vascular, renal, haematological, and systemic phenotypes that overlap with immune-mediated disorders. This Review critically examines the boundary between infection-driven immunopathology and autoimmunity. Hantavirus infection involves endothelial dysfunction, innate antiviral sensing, interferon responses, cytokine and chemokine amplification, complement activation, immunothrombosis, and adaptive immune responses, whose expression is further influenced by virus-, host-, and tissue-specific factors. These mechanisms contribute to the marked heterogeneity of clinical disease and may reproduce autoimmune-like clinical and laboratory features, but do not, in themselves, establish sustained loss of immune tolerance. Current evidence is substantially stronger for acute infection-associated immunopathology and phenotypic mimicry than for persistent autoreactivity or causally attributable post-infectious autoimmune disease. Reported autoimmune phenomena remain uncommon and predominantly case-based, while longitudinal, antigen-specific, and epidemiological evidence is limited. Persistent or recurrent manifestations after resolution of infection therefore require additional serological, histopathological, or other objective evidence of sustained immune dysregulation. Hantavirus infection thus provides a clinically informative model for distinguishing antiviral immunopathology from autoimmune-like mimicry and rare possible post-infectious autoimmunity, with direct implications for diagnosis and therapeutic restraint. 啮齿动物和人类安第斯病毒之间的单一氨基酸差异会影响病毒糖蛋白的运输。

安第斯病毒 (ANDV) 会导致汉坦病毒肺综合征 (HPS)，很少有爆发涉及人与人之间的传播。从一次此类疫情中分离出来的人类 ANDV 和来自同一地区的啮齿动物分离物对叙利亚金仓鼠的致死率不同。基因组分析揭示了 25 个非同义替换，其中一个导致人类 ANDV 糖蛋白前体在其切割位点附近发生 T641I 变化。尽管分离株的生长动力学不同，但链特异性 RT-qPCR 并不支持病毒 RNA 合成的重大差异，而人类分离株的病毒体释放则延迟。在人类细胞表达系统中，与野生型糖蛋白相比，人类 ANDV 糖蛋白中的单个 I641T 取代导致高尔基体保留减少和病毒粒子样颗粒的释放更快。由于 ANDV 流出发生在高尔基体，因此人类 ANDV 分离株中存在的 T641I 变化可能代表影响糖蛋白运输和病毒颗粒释放的人类适应性突变。

A single-amino acid difference between rodent and human Andes virus affects viral glycoprotein trafficking.

Andes virus (ANDV) causes hantavirus pulmonary syndrome (HPS), with rare outbreaks involving person-to-person transmission. A human ANDV isolate from one such outbreak and a rodent isolate from the same region differ in lethality in Syrian golden hamsters. Genomic analysis revealed 25 nonsynonymous substitutions, including one leading to a T641I change in the human ANDV glycoprotein precursor near its cleavage site. Although the isolates differed in growth kinetics, strand-specific RT-qPCR did not support major differences in viral RNA synthesis, whereas virion release was delayed in the case of the human isolate. In human cell expression systems, a single I641T substitution in the human ANDV glycoprotein resulted in decreased Golgi retention and faster release of virion-like particles relative to wild-type glycoprotein. Since ANDV egress takes place at the Golgi, the T641I change present in the human ANDV isolate may represent a human adaptive mutation influencing glycoprotein trafficking and virus particle release.

Hantavirus Outbreak Caused by Andes Virus Linked to International Cruise Ship Travel - United States, 2026.

Andes virus (ANDV), the only hantavirus known to be transmitted person-to-person, is endemic to areas of South America and can cause hantavirus pulmonary syndrome, which has a case-fatality rate of approximately 40%. On May 4, 2026, CDC was notified of seven cases of severe acute respiratory illness, including three deaths, among 147 persons aboard a cruise ship in the Atlantic Ocean; the illnesses were later confirmed to be caused by ANDV. No cases occurred among passengers who were U.S. residents. Ten of 28 U.S. resident passengers disembarked before ANDV infection was confirmed aboard the ship. On May 10, the remaining 18 U.S. resident passengers were repatriated to the National Quarantine Unit in Omaha, Nebraska, for risk assessment and monitoring, and where optimized advanced care was available, should symptoms develop. CDC and U.S. health departments monitored 60 additional U.S. residents potentially exposed on flights involving a passenger with symptomatic ANDV infection from the cruise, totaling 88 contacts across 27 jurisdictions. All 88 contacts completed 42 days of monitoring, with no secondary ANDV infections identified. Worldwide, local health authorities assessed and monitored approximately 653 potentially exposed persons from 33 countries. This response highlights the value of rapid coordination among partners to locate and monitor potentially exposed persons during an international outbreak to reduce the risk for ANDV transmission. Prompt notification to public health authorities can facilitate testing, patient care, contact identification, and implementation of measures to limit secondary transmission.
