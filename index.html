巴塔哥尼亚猛禽体内维生素 K1 耗尽证实抗凝血灭鼠剂中毒。

第二代抗凝血灭鼠剂（SGAR）的使用引发了持久性生物累积性有毒物质（PBT）监管的困境，因为这些产品的社会需求量很大，例如控制人畜共患害虫，但没有明确的替代品。在这项工作中，我们首次描述了阿根廷和南美洲猛禽中的 SGAR 污染。我们发现肝脏中的水平和 SGAR（溴敌隆、溴敌隆、芬那考姆、氟古马芬和敌噻酮）的生物累积率（18.2%）令人担忧，特别是在阿根廷西北部巴塔哥尼亚的城市地区，那里感染安第斯山脉病毒的风险很高，并且使用 SGAR 来控制啮齿动物很常见。鉴于在个体水平上诊断 SGAR 中毒的困难，我们开发了一种新方法来测量肝脏维生素 K1 浓度并结合 SGAR 残留分析。使用这种方法，我们证实具有 SGAR 残留的鸟类表现出较低的肝脏维生素 K1 浓度，从而开启了使用该生物标记物来支持 SGAR 中毒诊断的可能性。一只小角鸮 (Bubo magellanicus) 的情况就是这样，它有凝血障碍的迹象，肝脏中 SGAR 总量为 1.2 µgg-1，维生素 K1 水平完全耗尽。从长远来看，在安第斯病毒感染高风险地区使用 SGAR 可能会适得其反，因为对 SGAR 具有抗性的啮齿动物往往会被选择，而它们的捕食者则会遭受 PBT 的预期不利影响，从而导致丧失通过化学和自然方式控制啮齿动物种群的能力。

Anticoagulant rodenticide poisoning confirmed by vitamin K1 depletion in raptors from Patagonia.

The use of second-generation anticoagulant rodenticides (SGARs) raises a dilemma regarding the regulation of persistent bioaccumulative toxic substances (PBTs), given that these products have applications in high demand by society, such as the control of zoonotic pests, for which there are no clear alternatives. In this work, we describe for the first time SGAR contamination in birds of prey from Argentina and South America. We found concerning levels in the liver and prevalence of SGAR (brodifacoum, bromadiolone, difenacoum, flocoumafen and difethialone) bioaccumulation (18.2%), particularly around urban areas of NW Argentine Patagonia, where the risk of Andes Virus infection is high and the use of SGARs for rodent control is common. Given the difficulty of diagnosing SGAR poisoning at the individual level, we developed a novel method to measure hepatic vitamin K1 concentration coupled with SGAR residue analysis. Using this method, we confirmed that birds with SGAR residues showed lower hepatic concentrations of vitamin K1, opening the possibility of using this biomarker to support the diagnosis of SGAR poisoning. This was the case for a Lesser Horned Owl (Bubo magellanicus) with signs of coagulopathy, 1.2 µg g-1 of total SGARs in the liver, and a complete depletion of vitamin K1 levels. The use of SGARs in areas at high risk of Andes Virus infection may be counterproductive in the long term, as SGAR-resistant rodents tend to be selected while their predators experience the expected adverse effects of PBTs, leading to a loss of the capacity to control rodent populations, both chemically and naturally.

针对新兴病毒的呼吸道粘膜疫苗：前景与挑战。

系统注射疫苗有助于减少 SARS-CoV-2 大流行期间的严重疾病、住院治疗和死亡。然而，它们常常无法始终如一地在粘膜部位引发强大的免疫力。这篇小型综述探讨了呼吸道粘膜免疫在抵御新出现的病毒病原体方面的核心作用，并强调了 SARS-CoV-2 大流行的关键要点。 SARS-CoV-2 和流感研究的证据表明，粘膜疫苗接种可以独特地诱导分泌性 IgA 以及上、下呼吸道内的常驻记忆 B 和 T 细胞，从而促进更广泛和更有效的保护。因此，最近重新燃起了对粘膜疫苗接种策略的兴趣。然而，仍然存在显着的知识差距，其中包括确定最佳疫苗接种策略（全身初粘膜加强疫苗接种与仅粘膜疫苗接种），确定导致粘膜免疫相对快速衰减的潜在机制，建立可重复和标准化的保护相关性，以及开发与呼吸环境兼容的安全、有效的递送平台和佐剂。这些挑战与高度优先的人畜共患威胁尤其相关，例如亨尼帕病毒、汉坦病毒、沙粒病毒和新出现的流感病毒株，这些病毒的粘膜免疫反应和保护的相关性仍然不明确。此外，通过改进病毒载体、纳米粒子系统和免疫调节佐剂来推进粘膜疫苗设计对于实现持久免疫至关重要。最终，利用从 SARS-CoV-2 大流行中获得的见解可能会在粘膜疫苗接种策略方面取得突破，从而减少传播、限制病毒进化并加强对未来呼吸道大流行的准备。

Respiratory mucosal vaccines for emerging viruses: promise and challenges.

Systemically administered vaccines were instrumental in reducing severe disease, hospitalizations, and deaths during the SARS-CoV-2 pandemic. However, they often failed to consistently elicit robust immunity at mucosal sites. This minireview examines a central role for respiratory mucosal immunity in protection against emerging viral pathogens and highlights key takeaways from the SARS-CoV-2 pandemic. Evidence from SARS-CoV-2 and influenza studies demonstrates that mucosal vaccination uniquely induces secretory IgA, as well as resident memory B and T cells within the upper and lower airways, thereby promoting broader and more potent protection. Accordingly, interest in mucosal vaccination strategies has been recently renewed. However, significant knowledge gaps remain, which include determining optimal vaccination strategies (systemic prime-mucosal boost versus mucosal-only), identifying the underlying mechanisms that cause the relatively rapid decay of mucosal immunity, establishing reproducible and standardized correlates of protection, and developing safe, effective delivery platforms and adjuvants that are compatible with the respiratory environment. These challenges are particularly relevant for high-priority zoonotic threats, such as henipaviruses, hantaviruses, arenaviruses, and emerging influenza strains, for which mucosal immune responses and correlates of protection remain poorly defined. Moreover, advancing mucosal vaccine design through improved viral vectors, nanoparticle systems, and immunomodulatory adjuvants will be critical for achieving durable immunity. Ultimately, leveraging insights gained from the SARS-CoV-2 pandemic may enable breakthroughs in mucosal vaccination strategies that reduce transmission, limit viral evolution, and strengthen preparedness for future respiratory pandemics.

下一次病毒大流行的潜在特征：呼吸道传播的作用。

准确确定下一次大流行将会是什么是很复杂的，但是，通过研究以前的病毒大流行的一些共同特征，我们可以预测下一次大流行的一般特征。在这里，我们将探讨下一次大流行的特征，回顾过去困扰人类的大流行，以及可能使一种病毒病原体比其他病毒病原体更有可能引起未来大流行的因素。加速流行病发生的因素有很多，包括气候变化、现代世界中人类频繁的互动以及病毒储存库对自然环境的威胁。通过研究一系列要求，其中最重要的是病毒可以通过呼吸和空气传播，并且能够在人与人之间传播，以及其进行基因突变的能力，我们已经确定了一组可能成为未来流行病的特定病毒。

Potential features of the next viral pandemic: the role of respiratory transmission.

Determining exactly what the next pandemic will be is complex, However, by studying some common characteristics of previous viral pandemics, we can predict the general characteristics of the next pandemic. Here, we will explore next pandemic features, reviewing the past pandemics that have afflicted humanity and the factors that might make one viral pathogen more likely than others to cause a future pandemic. There are many factors that accelerate the occurrence of pandemics, including climate change, intensive human interaction in our modern world, and the threat to natural environments of viral reservoirs. By studying a set of requirements, the most important of which is that the virus be transmitted through respiration and air, and that it be able to be transmitted between humans, as well as its ability to undergo genetic mutations, we have identified a specific set of viruses that could potentially turn into future pandemics.

气候变化和全球流动时代欧洲的汉坦病毒风险和准备情况。

汉坦病毒是啮齿类动物传播的人畜共患病，可引起肾综合征出血热 (HFRS) 和汉坦病毒心肺综合征 (HCPS)。在欧洲，普马拉病毒和多布拉瓦病毒是最常见的疾病；首尔病毒与港口和城市环境有关。 2026 年 MV Hondius 游轮出现安第斯汉坦病毒 (ANDV) 感染群，涉及来自 23 个国家的乘客，凸显了欧洲在地方性和输入性汉坦病毒病防范方面的紧迫差距。回顾欧洲汉坦病毒的流行病学、生态学以及临床识别、诊断和防范，重点关注地方性普马拉病毒 (PUUV)、多布拉瓦病毒 (DOBV) 和首尔病毒 (SEOV)，并阐明输入性安第斯病毒 (ANDV) 感染与旅行医学和海上应对的单独相关性。我们对 2000 年 1 月至 2026 年 5 月发表的文献进行了结构化叙述性综述，优先考虑监测报告、系统性和叙述性综述、啮齿动物生态学研究、诊断评估、外部质量评估研究和疫情调查。还审查了 WHO、ECDC 和《国际卫生条例》指导文件。欧洲汉坦病毒流行病学是异质性的，并受到储存生态学、生物群落特异性啮齿动物动态、临床意识、诊断能力和监测实践的影响。气候影响是间接的且因地区而异，由肥大年份、捕食者-猎物动态、冬季条件、土地利用、水库密度和人类暴露程度介导。诊断准备情况参差不齐，尤其是分子检测方面。输入性 ANDV 感染在欧洲很少见，但与旅行医学和高后果准备有关，因为它可引起严重的汉坦病毒心肺综合征，并且与欧洲地方性汉坦病毒不同，已记录人际传播。欧洲的准备工作应通过啮齿动物生物监测、统一监测、改进分子诊断和本地校准早期预警，优先考虑 PUUV、DOBV 和 SEOV。

Hantavirus risk and preparedness in Europe in an era of climate change and global mobility.

Hantaviruses are rodent-borne zoonoses causing haemorrhagic fever with renal syndrome (HFRS) and hantavirus cardiopulmonary syndrome (HCPS). In Europe, Puumala virus and Dobrava virus account for most recognised disease; Seoul virus is relevant to ports and urban settings. The 2026 MV Hondius cruise-ship cluster of Andes hantavirus (ANDV) infections, involving passengers from 23 countries, has highlighted urgent gaps in European preparedness for both endemic and imported hantavirus disease. To review hantavirus epidemiology, ecology, and clinical recognition, diagnostics, and preparedness in Europe, with emphasis on endemic Puumala virus (PUUV), Dobrava virus (DOBV), and Seoul virus (SEOV), and to clarify the separate relevance of imported Andes virus (ANDV) infection for travel medicine and maritime response. We conducted a structured narrative review of literature published from January 2000 to May 2026, prioritising surveillance reports, systematic and narrative reviews, rodent ecology studies, diagnostic evaluations, external quality assessment studies, and outbreak investigations. WHO, ECDC, and International Health Regulations guidance documents were also reviewed. European hantavirus epidemiology is heterogeneous and shaped by reservoir ecology, biome-specific rodent dynamics, clinical awareness, diagnostic capacity, and surveillance practice. Climate effects are indirect and regionally variable, mediated by mast years, predator-prey dynamics, winter conditions, land use, reservoir density, and human exposure. Diagnostic preparedness is uneven, particularly for molecular detection. Imported ANDV infection is rare in Europe but relevant to travel medicine and high-consequence preparedness because it can cause severe hantavirus cardiopulmonary syndrome and, unlike endemic European hantaviruses, has documented person-to-person transmission. European preparedness should prioritise PUUV, DOBV, and SEOV through rodent biomonitoring, harmonised surveillance, improved molecular diagnostics, and locally calibrated early warning.
