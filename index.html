HFRS 的表观遗传学：将免疫、发病机制和疫苗诱导的保护联系起来。

肾综合征出血热 (HFRS) 由旧世界汉坦病毒引起，包括汉坦病毒 (HTNV) 和首尔病毒 (SEOV)，是一个重要的公共卫生问题，特别是在亚洲。临床表现包括发热性疾病、血管渗漏、血小板减少、出血和急性肾损伤。疾病严重程度变化以及疫苗诱导免疫的变异性和持久性的潜在机制仍不完全清楚。表观遗传机制，包括 DNA 甲基化、组蛋白修饰、染色质调节和非编码 RNA 介导的调节，可以影响多种生物环境中的抗病毒免疫和免疫细胞功能；然而，它们对 HFRS 的具体贡献仍未得到充分表征。这篇结构化叙述性综述检查了汉坦病毒特异性证据以及其他病毒感染和疫苗接种环境的相关发现，重点关注炎症反应、内皮功能障碍、调节性非编码 RNA、训练有素的免疫和疫苗学中的潜在应用。 HTNV 实验研究和调节 RNA 的临床研究已经确定了与汉坦病毒-宿主相互作用相关的候选途径；然而，这些发现并没有证实表观遗传机制决定 HFRS 的严重程度或临床结果。同样，临床前汉坦病毒疫苗研究和早期临床评估提供了有关免疫原性和安全性的证据，但没有证明保护性免疫的表观遗传介导。因此，主要与汉坦病毒心肺综合征 (HCPS) 相关的安第斯病毒 (ANDV) 和其他病毒系统的发现被认为是比较或背景证据，不能直接推广到 HFRS。这篇综述确定了关键的知识差距，并概述了纵向、细胞解析的表观基因组分析、功能验证和综合多组学如何阐明因果机制，解释疾病结果和疫苗反应的个体间差异，并确定表观遗传生物标志物和干预措施的潜在转化价值。

Epigenetics of HFRS: Linking Immunity, Pathogenesis, and Vaccine-Induced Protection.

Haemorrhagic fever with renal syndrome (HFRS), caused by Old World hantaviruses, including Hantaan virus (HTNV) and Seoul virus (SEOV), is an important public-health concern, particularly in Asia. Clinical manifestations range from febrile illness to vascular leakage, thrombocytopenia, haemorrhage and acute kidney injury. The mechanisms underlying variation in disease severity and the variability and durability of vaccine-induced immunity remain incompletely understood. Epigenetic mechanisms, including DNA methylation, histone modifications, chromatin regulation and non-coding RNA-mediated regulation, can influence antiviral immunity and immune-cell function in several biological settings; however, their specific contributions to HFRS remain insufficiently characterized. This structured narrative review examines hantavirus-specific evidence alongside relevant findings from other viral infections and vaccination settings, focusing on inflammatory responses, endothelial dysfunction, regulatory non-coding RNAs, trained immunity and potential applications in vaccinology. Experimental HTNV studies and clinical investigations of regulatory RNAs have identified candidate pathways associated with hantavirus-host interactions; however, these findings do not establish that epigenetic mechanisms determine HFRS severity or clinical outcomes. Similarly, preclinical hantavirus vaccine studies and early clinical evaluations provide evidence regarding immunogenicity and safety but do not demonstrate epigenetic mediation of protective immunity. Findings from Andes virus (ANDV), associated primarily with hantavirus cardiopulmonary syndrome (HCPS), and other viral systems are therefore considered comparative or contextual evidence, not directly generalizable to HFRS. This review identifies critical knowledge gaps and outlines how longitudinal, cell-resolved epigenomic profiling, functional validation and integrated multi-omics could clarify causal mechanisms, explain interindividual variation in disease outcomes and vaccine responses, and establish the potential translational value of epigenetic biomarkers and interventions.

新世界汉坦病毒识别原钙粘蛋白-1 的结构基础。

汉坦病毒是啮齿动物传播的病原体，可引起严重的人类疾病，目前尚无批准的治疗方法。新世界汉坦病毒，包括安第斯病毒 (ANDV)，利用细胞表面受体原钙粘蛋白-1 (PCDH1) 进入宿主细胞1。然而，这种相互作用的结构基础仍不清楚。在这里，我们描述了以近原子分辨率与 ANDV 尖峰结合的 PCDH1 的结构。 PCDH1 不对称地接合由刺突中的四个 Gn 原聚体形成的深中央口袋，这解释了为什么只有完整的病毒颗粒或可溶性四聚体才能识别它。诱变鉴定出三个 Gn 环，它们塑造受体结合位点并提供 PCDH1 结合从新世界糖蛋白到旧世界糖蛋白的转移。对四种中和抗体的结构和功能分析表明，它们通过两种不同的机制阻断受体结合——阻断进入 PCDH1 结合位点或在空间上阻止受体结合。总之，这些发现定义了汉坦病毒受体识别的结构决定因素，揭示了病毒如何通过将其凹陷在表面凹陷中来掩盖其受体结合位点，以及抗体如何对抗这一策略，并为设计针对汉坦病毒刺突的抗病毒疗法提供了框架。

Structural basis for protocadherin-1 recognition by New World hantaviruses.

Hantaviruses are rodent-borne pathogens that cause severe human disease for which no approved therapeutics are available. New World hantaviruses, including Andes virus (ANDV), use the cell-surface receptor protocadherin-1 (PCDH1) to enter host cells1. However, the structural basis of this interaction remains unclear. Here we describe the structure of PCDH1 bound to the ANDV spike at near-atomic resolution. PCDH1 asymmetrically engages a deep central pocket formed by four Gn protomers in the spike, explaining why only intact viral particles or soluble tetramers recognize it. Mutagenesis identified three Gn loops that shape the receptor-binding site and afford the transfer of PCDH1 binding from New World to Old World glycoproteins. Structural and functional analyses of four neutralizing antibodies revealed that they block receptor binding through two distinct mechanisms-occluding access to the PCDH1-binding site or sterically preventing receptor engagement. Together, these findings define the structural determinants of hantavirus-receptor recognition, reveal how a virus can mask its receptor-binding site by recessing it in a surface depression and how antibodies can counter this strategy, and provide a framework for the design of antiviral therapeutics targeting the hantavirus spike.

汉坦病毒作为新兴的人畜共患病病原体：分子生物学、流行病学、治疗学、疫苗和发病机制。

汉坦病毒是全球分布的人畜共患病原体，属于汉坦病毒科，导致两种主要人类疾病：肾综合征出血热（HFRS）和汉坦病毒心肺综合征（HCPS）。尽管汉坦病毒研究取得了实质性进展，但在理解病毒进化、储存库生态、环境变化、传播动力学和宿主免疫病理学之间的相互作用方面仍然存在重要挑战。这篇综述提供了一种综合的“同一个健康”视角，将分子病毒学和宿主病毒相互作用与传播生态学、全球流行病学以及血管渗漏和严重疾病的机制联系起来。除了总结现有知识外，我们还重点介绍了汉坦病毒分类学、基因组和分子监测、生态和传播模型以及识别先前未被认识的地区汉坦病毒循环的最新进展。特别强调储存宿主动态、气候和环境变化、栖息地干扰以及人类与野生动物相互作用在影响溢出风险中的作用，以及内皮功能障碍、免疫失调和凝血异常作为疾病严重程度的相互关联机制。我们还确定了有关宿主易感性、病毒持久性、毒力差异和组织向性的决定因素、种间传播以及有效抗病毒疗法和疫苗开发等方面持续存在的知识差距。通过整合这些互补的观点，本综述为了解汉坦病毒的出现和发病机制提供了一个框架，同时确定了未来监测、预防和治疗研究的优先事项。

Hantaviruses as Emerging Zoonotic Pathogens: Molecular Biology, Epidemiology, Therapeutics, vaccine, and Pathogenesis.

Hantaviruses are globally distributed zoonotic pathogens belonging to the family Hantaviridae and are responsible for two major human diseases, hemorrhagic fever with renal syndrome (HFRS) and hantavirus cardiopulmonary syndrome (HCPS). Despite substantial progress in hantavirus research, important challenges remain in understanding the interactions among viral evolution, reservoir ecology, environmental change, transmission dynamics, and host immunopathology. This review provides an integrated One Health perspective that connects molecular virology and host-virus interactions with transmission ecology, global epidemiology, and the mechanisms underlying vascular leakage and severe disease. In addition to summarizing established knowledge, we highlight recent advances in hantavirus taxonomy, genomic and molecular surveillance, ecological and transmission modeling, and the identification of hantavirus circulation in previously underrecognized regions. Particular emphasis is placed on the role of reservoir host dynamics, climate and environmental change, habitat disturbance, and human-wildlife interactions in shaping spillover risk, as well as on endothelial dysfunction, immune dysregulation, and coagulation abnormalities as interconnected mechanisms of disease severity. We also identify persistent knowledge gaps concerning host susceptibility, viral persistence, determinants of differential virulence and tissue tropism, interspecies transmission, and the development of effective antiviral therapies and vaccines. By integrating these complementary perspectives, this review provides a framework for understanding hantavirus emergence and pathogenesis while identifying priorities for future surveillance, prevention, and therapeutic research.

原钙粘蛋白-1 用途的扩展揭示了汉坦病毒更广泛的进入领域。

哺乳动物汉坦病毒是RNA病毒，在美洲引起汉坦病毒心肺综合征，在欧亚大陆引起肾综合征出血热。大多数汉坦病毒的细胞进入机制仍然不明确。为了检查在非洲、欧亚大陆和美洲发现的系统发育上不同的汉坦病毒的进入，我们生成了具有复制能力的重组水泡性口炎病毒（rVSV），其带有 Necoclí、Sangassou、Thottapalayam、Kenkeme、Nova、Oxbow 和 Tula 病毒的 Gn/Gc 蛋白。除 Kenkeme 外，所有这些 Gn/Gc 蛋白均支持原代人内皮细胞的感染，表明内皮细胞允许的汉坦病毒范围比以前认识的更广泛。除了 rVSV-Kenkeme 之外，这些 rVSV 在抢救和传代过程中除了预先设计的增强抢救的变化外，没有获得额外的突变。对缺乏原钙粘蛋白-1 (PCDH1) 的人类细胞进行的基因研究表明，Necoclí、Tula 和 Nova 病毒利用 PCDH1 进行有效感染，尽管 Nova 表型较弱。这三种Gn/Gc蛋白以不同的表观亲合力结合可溶性PCDH1，并且可溶性PCDH1抑制相应rVSV的感染； Necoclí 和 Tula（但不是 Nova）也被 PCDH1 靶向单克隆抗体阻断。在 PCDH1 敲除的内皮细胞中，真正的图拉病毒感染也同样减少。最后，具有广泛反应性的抗 Gn/Gc 人单克隆抗体 ADI-42898 可有效中和 Necoclí、Nova、Sangassou 和 Kenkeme rVSV，但对 Oxbow、Tula 和 Thottapalayam rVSV 的活性较弱或无法检测到。总之，这些发现扩大了能够介导人内皮细胞感染的汉坦病毒糖蛋白的范围，拓宽了 PCDH1 依赖性进入的系统发育范围，并确定了具有差异活性的受体靶向和病毒糖蛋白靶向策略。 重要性许多新发现的汉坦病毒仅从序列数据中得知，而它们进入人类细胞的能力及其受体的使用尚未解决。使用 BSL2 兼容的重组水泡性口炎病毒 (rVSV) 系统，我们发现来自几种不同的汉坦病毒的糖蛋白可以介导原代人内皮细胞的感染，这表明内皮细胞进入的许可范围比以前想象的更广泛。我们还表明，以前主要与新世界汉坦病毒相关的原钙粘蛋白-1 (PCDH1) 被 Necoclí、Tula 和 Nova 病毒使用，但在整个组中并不普遍，揭示了更广泛但异质的受体使用。真实的图拉病毒实验在替代系统之外支持了这一结论。最后，一种具有广泛反应性的抗 Gn/Gc 抗体可以中和其中几种病毒，但不是全部，这凸显了广泛保护性对策的前景和局限性，以及这些 rVSV 在评估进入抑制剂方面的效用。

Expanded protocadherin-1 usage reveals a broader hantavirus entry landscape.

Mammalian hantaviruses are RNA viruses that cause hantavirus cardiopulmonary syndrome in the Americas and hemorrhagic fever with renal syndrome in Eurasia. The cellular entry mechanisms of most hantaviruses remain poorly defined. To examine entry by phylogenetically distinct hantaviruses identified across Africa, Eurasia, and the Americas, we generated replication-competent recombinant vesicular stomatitis viruses (rVSVs) bearing Gn/Gc proteins from Necoclí, Sangassou, Thottapalayam, Kenkeme, Nova, Oxbow, and Tula viruses. All these Gn/Gc proteins except Kenkeme supported infection of primary human endothelial cells, indicating that endothelial cell entry is permissive for a broader range of hantaviruses than previously appreciated. Except for rVSV-Kenkeme, these rVSVs did not acquire additional mutations beyond pre-engineered rescue-enhancing changes during rescue and passaging. Genetic studies in human cells lacking protocadherin-1 (PCDH1) showed that Necoclí, Tula, and Nova viruses use PCDH1 for efficient infection, although the Nova phenotype was weaker. These three Gn/Gc proteins bound soluble PCDH1 with different apparent avidities, and infection by the corresponding rVSVs was inhibited by soluble PCDH1; Necoclí and Tula, but not Nova, were also blocked by a PCDH1-targeting monoclonal antibody. Authentic Tula virus infection was similarly reduced in PCDH1 knockout endothelial cells. Finally, the broadly reactive anti-Gn/Gc human monoclonal antibody ADI-42898 efficiently neutralized Necoclí, Nova, Sangassou, and Kenkeme rVSVs, but showed weak or undetectable activity against Oxbow, Tula, and Thottapalayam rVSVs. Together, these findings expand the range of hantavirus glycoproteins capable of mediating infection of human endothelial cells, broaden the phylogenetic scope of PCDH1-dependent entry, and identify receptor-targeted and viral glycoprotein-targeted strategies with differential activity.IMPORTANCEMany newly discovered hantaviruses are known only from sequence data, leaving their ability to enter human cells and their receptor usage unresolved. Using a BSL2-compatible recombinant vesicular stomatitis virus (rVSV) system, we show that glycoproteins from several divergent hantaviruses can mediate infection of primary human endothelial cells, indicating that endothelial cell entry is permissive for a broader range of hantaviruses than previously appreciated. We also show that protocadherin-1 (PCDH1), previously linked mainly to New World hantaviruses, is used by Necoclí, Tula, and Nova viruses but not universally across the panel, revealing broader but heterogeneous receptor usage. An authentic Tula virus experiment supports this conclusion beyond the surrogate system. Finally, a broadly reactive anti-Gn/Gc antibody neutralizes several, but not all, of these viruses, highlighting both the promise and the limits of broadly protective countermeasures and the utility of these rVSVs for evaluating entry inhibitors.
