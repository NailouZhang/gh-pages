原钙粘蛋白-1 用途的扩展揭示了汉坦病毒更广泛的进入领域。

哺乳动物汉坦病毒是RNA病毒，在美洲引起汉坦病毒心肺综合征，在欧亚大陆引起肾综合征出血热。大多数汉坦病毒的细胞进入机制仍然不明确。为了检查在非洲、欧亚大陆和美洲发现的系统发育上不同的汉坦病毒的进入，我们生成了具有复制能力的重组水泡性口炎病毒（rVSV），其带有 Necoclí、Sangassou、Thottapalayam、Kenkeme、Nova、Oxbow 和 Tula 病毒的 Gn/Gc 蛋白。除 Kenkeme 外，所有这些 Gn/Gc 蛋白均支持原代人内皮细胞的感染，表明内皮细胞允许的汉坦病毒范围比以前认识的更广泛。除了 rVSV-Kenkeme 之外，这些 rVSV 在抢救和传代过程中除了预先设计的增强抢救的变化外，没有获得额外的突变。对缺乏原钙粘蛋白-1 (PCDH1) 的人类细胞进行的基因研究表明，Necoclí、Tula 和 Nova 病毒利用 PCDH1 进行有效感染，尽管 Nova 表型较弱。这三种Gn/Gc蛋白以不同的表观亲合力结合可溶性PCDH1，并且可溶性PCDH1抑制相应rVSV的感染； Necoclí 和 Tula（但不是 Nova）也被 PCDH1 靶向单克隆抗体阻断。在 PCDH1 敲除的内皮细胞中，真正的图拉病毒感染也同样减少。最后，具有广泛反应性的抗 Gn/Gc 人单克隆抗体 ADI-42898 可有效中和 Necoclí、Nova、Sangassou 和 Kenkeme rVSV，但对 Oxbow、Tula 和 Thottapalayam rVSV 的活性较弱或无法检测到。总之，这些发现扩大了能够介导人内皮细胞感染的汉坦病毒糖蛋白的范围，拓宽了 PCDH1 依赖性进入的系统发育范围，并确定了具有差异活性的受体靶向和病毒糖蛋白靶向策略。 重要性许多新发现的汉坦病毒仅从序列数据中得知，而它们进入人类细胞的能力及其受体的使用尚未解决。使用 BSL2 兼容的重组水泡性口炎病毒 (rVSV) 系统，我们发现来自几种不同的汉坦病毒的糖蛋白可以介导原代人内皮细胞的感染，这表明内皮细胞进入的许可范围比以前想象的更广泛。我们还表明，以前主要与新世界汉坦病毒相关的原钙粘蛋白-1 (PCDH1) 被 Necoclí、Tula 和 Nova 病毒使用，但在整个组中并不普遍，揭示了更广泛但异质的受体使用。真实的图拉病毒实验在替代系统之外支持了这一结论。最后，一种具有广泛反应性的抗 Gn/Gc 抗体可以中和其中几种病毒，但不是全部，这凸显了广泛保护性对策的前景和局限性，以及这些 rVSV 在评估进入抑制剂方面的效用。

Expanded protocadherin-1 usage reveals a broader hantavirus entry landscape.

Mammalian hantaviruses are RNA viruses that cause hantavirus cardiopulmonary syndrome in the Americas and hemorrhagic fever with renal syndrome in Eurasia. The cellular entry mechanisms of most hantaviruses remain poorly defined. To examine entry by phylogenetically distinct hantaviruses identified across Africa, Eurasia, and the Americas, we generated replication-competent recombinant vesicular stomatitis viruses (rVSVs) bearing Gn/Gc proteins from Necoclí, Sangassou, Thottapalayam, Kenkeme, Nova, Oxbow, and Tula viruses. All these Gn/Gc proteins except Kenkeme supported infection of primary human endothelial cells, indicating that endothelial cell entry is permissive for a broader range of hantaviruses than previously appreciated. Except for rVSV-Kenkeme, these rVSVs did not acquire additional mutations beyond pre-engineered rescue-enhancing changes during rescue and passaging. Genetic studies in human cells lacking protocadherin-1 (PCDH1) showed that Necoclí, Tula, and Nova viruses use PCDH1 for efficient infection, although the Nova phenotype was weaker. These three Gn/Gc proteins bound soluble PCDH1 with different apparent avidities, and infection by the corresponding rVSVs was inhibited by soluble PCDH1; Necoclí and Tula, but not Nova, were also blocked by a PCDH1-targeting monoclonal antibody. Authentic Tula virus infection was similarly reduced in PCDH1 knockout endothelial cells. Finally, the broadly reactive anti-Gn/Gc human monoclonal antibody ADI-42898 efficiently neutralized Necoclí, Nova, Sangassou, and Kenkeme rVSVs, but showed weak or undetectable activity against Oxbow, Tula, and Thottapalayam rVSVs. Together, these findings expand the range of hantavirus glycoproteins capable of mediating infection of human endothelial cells, broaden the phylogenetic scope of PCDH1-dependent entry, and identify receptor-targeted and viral glycoprotein-targeted strategies with differential activity.IMPORTANCEMany newly discovered hantaviruses are known only from sequence data, leaving their ability to enter human cells and their receptor usage unresolved. Using a BSL2-compatible recombinant vesicular stomatitis virus (rVSV) system, we show that glycoproteins from several divergent hantaviruses can mediate infection of primary human endothelial cells, indicating that endothelial cell entry is permissive for a broader range of hantaviruses than previously appreciated. We also show that protocadherin-1 (PCDH1), previously linked mainly to New World hantaviruses, is used by Necoclí, Tula, and Nova viruses but not universally across the panel, revealing broader but heterogeneous receptor usage. An authentic Tula virus experiment supports this conclusion beyond the surrogate system. Finally, a broadly reactive anti-Gn/Gc antibody neutralizes several, but not all, of these viruses, highlighting both the promise and the limits of broadly protective countermeasures and the utility of these rVSVs for evaluating entry inhibitors.

汉坦病毒肺综合征的血浆交换：未经测试的干预措施的机制案例。

汉坦病毒肺综合征 (HPS) 的病死率为 35%-40%，且尚无批准的药物治疗方法。其病理生理学根源于血浆室：细胞因子、病毒抗原、免疫复合物和血小板结合糖蛋白作为免疫病理性内皮损伤、非心源性肺水肿和心源性抑郁的驱动因素而循环。治疗性血浆置换 (TPE) 可实现血浆介质介质的大量清除，由美国血浆置换协会 (ASFA) 建立，用于具有类似机制原理的众多适应症。该评论认为，HPS 的免疫病理学映射到 TPE 的治疗靶点，任何干预都需要在早期心肺期进行，并且该疾病的凝血病维度为将血浆纳入替代策略提供了机制论据。我们特意将这一基本原理与类似病理生理学疾病中 TPE 的不均匀记录进行对比，在这些疾病中，干预措施在某些情况下有效，但在其他情况下没有明显的益处。最近，MV Hondius 号游轮上爆发了安第斯山脉病毒，造成了多国严重心肺病例，促使我们作为输血医学和单采医生阐明了这一假设。我们强调，这些论点是机械论和假设生成的，而不是基于证据的：尚无临床数据支持 HPS 中的 TPE，并且在得出任何关于疗效的结论之前，需要系统的结果报告（最好是通过多中心病例系列）。

Plasma Exchange for Hantavirus Pulmonary Syndrome: A Mechanistic Case for an Untested Intervention.

Hantavirus pulmonary syndrome (HPS) carries a case fatality rate of 35%-40% and has no approved pharmacologic treatment. Its pathophysiology is rooted in the plasma compartment: cytokines, viral antigens, immune complexes, and platelet-binding glycoproteins circulate as drivers of immunopathologic endothelial injury, noncardiogenic pulmonary edema, and cardiogenic depression. Therapeutic plasma exchange (TPE) achieves bulk clearance of plasma-borne mediators and is established by the American Society for Apheresis (ASFA) for numerous indications that share an analogous mechanistic rationale. This commentary argues that the immunopathology of HPS maps onto the therapeutic targets of TPE, that any intervention would need to be timed to the early cardiopulmonary phase, and that the coagulopathic dimension of the disease provides a mechanistic argument for incorporating plasma into the replacement strategy. We deliberately situate this rationale against the uneven record of TPE in diseases of comparable pathophysiology, in which the intervention has been effective in some settings and without demonstrable benefit in others. The recent Andes virus outbreak aboard the cruise ship MV Hondius, which produced a multinational cluster of severe cardiopulmonary cases, prompted us, as transfusion medicine and apheresis physicians, to articulate this hypothesis. We emphasize that these arguments are mechanistic and hypothesis-generating rather than evidence-based: no clinical data yet support TPE in HPS, and systematic outcome reporting, ideally through a multicenter case series, would be required before any conclusion about efficacy could be drawn.

以拉沙病毒核酸内切酶为例，对 XFEL 来源的药物筛选进行展望。

拉沙病毒（LASV）等新兴人类致病性RNA病毒以及汉坦病毒和安第斯病毒等密切相关的病毒仍然是全球主要的健康威胁。感染的治疗选择非常有限。因此，广泛保守的 LASV 抢帽核酸内切酶 (LASVendoN) 被用作我们的 X 射线化合物筛选实验的有趣药物靶标。考虑到仪器和连续晶体学方法开发的最新改进，可以使用不同的方法来促进药物化合物筛选。当前一代 X 射线自由电子激光 (XFEL) 源有望使用固定目标串行飞秒 X 射线晶体学获得更高质量的衍射数据并实现高样品通量。我们在直线加速器相干光源和同步加速器源 PETRA III 上收集了系列固定目标 X 射线衍射数据进行比较，并解析了与 LASVendoN 活性位点结合的 2,4-二氧代-4-苯基丁酸 (DPBA) 和巴洛沙韦酸 (BXA) 的晶体结构。这些已解决的结构代表了药物开发的合理起点。除了室温衍射数据外，还记录了100 K下的传统单晶衍射数据以进行比较。为了分析 XFEL 化合物筛选实验中的样品通量，我们进一步提供了细菌磷霉素抗性蛋白 A 的数据，该蛋白具有先前在同步加速器筛选实验中鉴定的 6 个配体。总之，XFEL 和同步加速器数据收集方法已被证明可用于化合物筛选，且在可实现的数据质量和分辨率方面存在微小差异。单独的实验装置、探测器以及可能在接近生理温度的 XFEL 上使用光束扫描，结合合理的样品消耗和最小化的辐射损伤，被认为有利于高通量药物的开发。

Perspectives for pharmaceutical screening at XFEL sources using the example of Lassa virus endonuclease.

Emerging human pathogenic RNA viruses such as Lassa virus (LASV) and closely related viruses such as Hantavirus and Andes virus continue to be a major health threat globally. Treatment options for infections are severely limited. The widely conserved cap-snatching endonuclease of LASV (LASVendoN) is therefore used as an interesting drug target for our X-ray compound-screening experiments. Considering recent improvements in instrumentation and serial crystallography method development, different approaches may be used to facilitate pharmaceutical compound screening. Current-generation X-ray free-electron laser (XFEL) sources hold promise for obtaining higher quality diffraction data and achieving a high sample throughput using fixed-target serial femtosecond X-ray crystallography. We collected serial fixed-target X-ray diffraction data at Linac Coherent Light Source and at the synchrotron source PETRA III for comparison and solved the crystal structures of 2,4-dioxo-4-phenylbutanoic acid (DPBA) and baloxavir acid (BXA) binding to the active site of LASVendoN. These solved structures represent a reasonable starting point for drug development. In addition to room-temperature diffraction data, conventional single-crystal diffraction data were recorded at 100 K for comparison. To analyse the sample throughput in a compound-screening experiment at an XFEL, we further present data for the bacterial fosfomycin-resistance protein A with six ligands previously identified in a synchrotron screening experiment. In summary, the XFEL and synchrotron data-collection approaches have proven to be useful for compound screening with minor differences in achievable data quality and resolution. The individual experimental setup, detector and potentially the use of beam sweeping at XFELs at close-to-physiological temperatures combined with reasonable sample consumption and minimized radiation damage are considered to be advantageous for high-throughput drug development.

汉坦病毒感染：日益严重的全球健康问题。

汉坦病毒越来越被视为全球健康的重要新兴风险，估计每年影响全球超过 20 万人。这些病原体是属于汉坦病毒科的单链RNA病毒，已知会引起人类两种主要的临床综合征：肾综合征出血热（HFRS）和汉坦病毒肺综合征（HPS）。与 HFRS 相关的所谓旧世界汉坦病毒主要分布在亚洲、欧洲和非洲部分地区。相比之下，导致 HPS 的新世界汉坦病毒主要局限于北美和南美。全球每年约有 150000-200000 例 HFRS 病例需要住院治疗，其中发展中地区的负担最大。 HFRS 的死亡率从低于 1% 到约 12% 不等，具体取决于感染菌株。这两种疾病形式的特点是毛细血管通透性增加，导致低血压、血小板减少和白细胞增多等临床特征。虽然 HFRS 的地理分布更广泛，但 HPS 的死亡风险更高。此外，新病毒株的发现和报告病例的稳步上升加剧了全球的担忧。因此，加强监测系统和扩大实验室诊断能力对于有效监测和控制汉坦病毒病至关重要。

Hantavirus infections: A growing global health concern.

Hantaviruses are increasingly regarded as an important emerging risk to global health, with an estimated annual impact on more than 200000 people worldwide. These pathogens are single-stranded RNA viruses belonging to the family Han taviridae and are known to cause two major clinical syndromes in humans: Hemorrhagic fever with renal syndrome (HFRS) and hantavirus pulmonary syndrome (HPS). The so-called old world hantaviruses, which are associated with HFRS, are predominantly distributed across Asia, Europe, and parts of Africa. In contrast, new world hantaviruses, responsible for HPS, are largely confined to North and South America. Each year, approximately 150000-200000 HFRS cases require hospitalization globally, with the greatest burden occurring in developing regions. The mortality rate of HFRS ranges from below 1% to about 12%, depending on the infecting strain. Both disease forms are characterized by increased capillary permeability, leading to clinical features such as hypotension, thrombocytopenia, and leukocytosis. While HFRS has a broader geographic distribution, HPS carries a higher risk of death. Moreover, the detection of new viral strains and the steady rise in reported cases have intensified global concern. Enhancing surveillance systems and expanding laboratory diagnostic capacity are therefore essential for effective monitoring and control of hantavirus diseases.

白蛉热病毒在斯里兰卡传播的新证据。

白蛉热病毒（SFFV）感染是一种由白蛉病毒引起的急性自限性发热性疾病，通过白蛉属白蛉传播。这种疾病已在地中海地区、北非、中东和印度次大陆报告过，目前被认为正在扩展到新的地理区域。临床上，SFFV 感染与急性登革热非常相似。检测 NS1 抗原血清学结果阴性、临床疑似登革热患者的其他可能感染，包括 SSFV。在这项研究中，从2023年1月至8月期间在斯里兰卡三家主要医院收治的临床疑似登革热患者总共收集了757份血清样本。患者来自科伦坡和康提地区，人口统计信息是从实验室申请表中获得的，尽管由于这项基于实验室的研究中的匿名化，详细的个人人口统计数据受到限制。所有样本均初步筛查登革热 NS1 抗原阴性，并进一步检测一组病原体，包括 SFFV 免疫球蛋白 (Ig) M、汉坦病毒 IgM、基孔肯雅热 IgM 和钩端螺旋体 IgM 抗体，以及汉坦病毒、黄病毒和甲病毒 RNA 的分子检测。使用商业免疫印迹检测，检测样本中的两个样本对 SFFV 特异性 IgM 呈血清学阳性，而登革热、基孔肯雅热、钩端螺旋体、汉坦病毒和相关 RNA 病毒的所有其他检测均为阴性。这两名 SFFV 阳性病例来自康提地区，包括一名儿童（6-8 岁）和一名成人（40-50 岁），两人均患有无并发症的登革热样疾病。这项研究提供了斯里兰卡 SFFV 感染的第一个证据。鉴于登革热在该国的高度流行，SFFV 感染可能在临床上被误诊为登革热，因此仍未得到充分认识。需要对发热患者和白蛉媒介监测进行进一步研究，以确定斯里兰卡 SFFV 的真实负担和流行病学意义。

Emerging evidence of sand fly fever virus transmission in Sri Lanka.

Sand fly fever virus (SFFV) infection is an acute self-limiting febrile illness caused by phleboviruses and transmitted by sand flies of the Phlebotomus species. The disease has been reported in the Mediterranean region, North Africa, the Middle East, and the Indian subcontinent, and is now considered to be expanding into new geographic areas. Clinically, SFFV infection closely resembles acute dengue fever. To detect other possible infection including SSFV in patients with clinically suspected dengue fever with negative serology results for NS1 antigen. In this study, a total of 757 serum samples were collected from patients clinically suspected of dengue fever and admitted to three major hospitals in Sri Lanka between January and August 2023. Patients were from the Colombo and Kandy District, and demographic information was obtained from laboratory request forms, although detailed individual-level demographic data were limited due to anonymization in this laboratory-based study. All samples were initially screened for dengue NS1 antigen negativity and further tested for a panel of pathogens including SFFV immunoglobulin (Ig) M, hantavirus IgM, chikungunya IgM, and leptospira IgM antibodies, as well as molecular detection of hantavirus, flavivirus, and alphavirus RNA. From tested samples, two samples were serologically positive for SFFV-specific IgM using a commercial immunoblot assay, while all other tests for dengue, chikungunya, leptospira, hantavirus, and related RNA viruses were negative. These two SFFV-positive cases were from Kandy District and included one child (6-8 years) and one adult (40-50 years), both presenting with uncomplicated dengue-like illness. This study provides the first evidence of SFFV infection in Sri Lanka. Given the high endemicity of dengue in the country, SFFV infections may be clinically misdiagnosed as dengue and therefore remain under-recognized. Further studies involving febrile patients and sand fly vector surveillance are required to determine the true burden and epidemiological significance of SFFV in Sri Lanka.

安第斯山脉病毒人际传播的特征和超级传播潜力。

通过使用历史接触者追踪数据，我们估计 23.4% 的病例引起了 80% 的安第斯病毒 (ANDV) 人际传播。我们证明，尽管 ANDV 的人际传播历来具有自限性，但在由密切接触者组成的无啮齿动物环境中观察到大规模 ANDV 感染爆发的可能性虽低，但不可忽略。

Characteristics and Superspreading Potential of Andes Virus Person-to-Person Transmission.

By using historical contact tracing data, we estimated that 23.4% of case-patients caused 80% of Andes virus (ANDV) person-to-person transmission. We demonstrated a low but nonnegligible probability of observing a large-scale ANDV infection outbreak in a rodent-free setting consisting of close contacts, despite the historically self-limited person-to-person transmission of ANDV.
