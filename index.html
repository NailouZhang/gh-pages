安第斯山脉病毒的人际传播：对当前证据及其临床意义的叙述性回顾。

汉坦病毒是一组导致新出现的人畜共患疾病的病毒，主要由啮齿类动物传播。安第斯病毒（ANDV）是南美洲流行的病毒，它在汉坦病毒中是独一无二的，因为有证据表明人与人之间的传播有限。本叙述性综述的目的是总结有关 ANDV 人际传播及其临床和流行病学意义的当前证据。在 PubMed/MEDLINE 和 Google Scholar 中进行了叙述性文献检索，包括流行病学研究、评论和实验报告。阿根廷和智利描述的疫情以及分子研究和动物模型都支持在密切和长期接触的环境中存在人际传播，特别是在疾病的早期阶段。唾液和呼吸道分泌物中病毒 RNA 的检测为这一现象提供了生物学依据。 ANDV 的传播性对流行地区的患者隔离、接触者追踪和流行病学监测具有相关意义。

Person-to-person transmission of Andes virus: a narrative review of current evidence and its clinical implications.

Hantaviruses constitute a group of viruses responsible for emerging zoonotic diseases primarily transmitted by rodents. Andes virus (ANDV), endemic to South America, is unique among hantaviruses because of evidence supporting limited person-to-person transmission. The aim of this narrative review is to summarize current evidence regarding interpersonal transmission of ANDV and its clinical and epidemiological implications. A narrative literature search was conducted in PubMed/MEDLINE and Google Scholar including epidemiological studies, reviews, and experimental reports. Outbreaks described in Argentina and Chile, together with molecular studies and animal models, support the existence of person-to-person transmission in settings involving close and prolonged contact, particularly during the early stages of disease. Detection of viral RNA in saliva and respiratory secretions provides biological plausibility for this phenomenon. The transmissibility of ANDV has relevant implications for patient isolation, contact tracing, and epidemiological surveillance in endemic regions.

Hantaviruses: Current insights, lessons from the MV Hondius outbreak, and an urgent call to action.

In May 2026, a cluster of laboratory-confirmed Andes virus (ANDV) infections was reported among passengers aboard the MV Hondius, a cruise ship that originated in Argentina and sailed through the South Atlantic. The exposed population included individuals from 23 countries and resulted in at least 13 cases, including three deaths, representing the first ANDV outbreak with a multi-continental range and providing further evidence consistent with human-to-human transmission of ANDV. There are currently no approved treatments or vaccines for ANDV, necessitating rapid development of drug and prophylactic pipelines. The outbreak highlighted the strengths and inherent weaknesses of our existing public health preparedness infrastructure in response to emerging infectious diseases. In this Pearl, we highlight the epidemiology, molecular virology, evolution, virus-host interactions, clinical management, therapeutic development, and public health response to ANDV. We emphasize the need to expand genomic surveillance, standardize diagnostic protocols across public health laboratories, and accelerate the clinical translation of promising antiviral candidates to strengthen pandemic preparedness infrastructure for understudied and emerging infectious diseases.

对韩国野生小型哺乳动物及其相关恙螨中的巴尔通体、伯内氏立克次体和正汉坦病毒进行分子监测。

Molecular surveillance of Bartonella species, Coxiella burnetii, and orthohantaviruses in wild small mammals and their associated chigger mites in South Korea.

Wild small mammals are important hosts of diverse zoonotic pathogens. Molecular surveillance incorporating both small mammals and their associated ectoparasites can provide information on pathogen detection at the wildlife-ectoparasite interface. In this study, 1,444 wild small mammals and 12,869 associated chigger mites, analyzed as 680 host-associated pools, were collected from 17 sites in South Korea between 2022 and 2025. Samples were examined for Bartonella spp., Coxiella burnetii, and orthohantaviruses using PCR- and RT-PCR-based methods, followed by sequence and phylogenetic analyses. The predominant small-mammal species, Apodemus agrarius, accounted for 84.9% of captured small mammals. DNA related to Bartonella schoenbuchensis was detected in 473 of 680 chigger mite pools (69.6%), whereas DNA related to Bartonella bacilliformis, Bartonella henselae, and Bartonella taylorii was detected in three of 1,444 small-mammal liver samples (0.2%) for each taxon. DNA of Coxiella burnetii was detected in 51 of 1,444 liver samples (3.5%), all collected during the autumn 2024 survey. Orthohantavirus RNA was detected in 37 small mammals (2.6%), predominantly in A. agrarius (35/37, 94.6%), with the remaining two detections in Crocidura spp. (2/37, 5.4%), and in three chigger mite pools (0.4%). To our knowledge, these findings provide the first molecular evidence of DNA related to B. schoenbuchensis in host-associated chigger mite pools and document previously unreported DNA related to B. bacilliformis and B. henselae in wild small-mammal liver samples in South Korea. The results provide descriptive baseline data for wildlife-based molecular surveillance; they do not establish seasonal patterns, individual-mite infection prevalence, or vector competence.

汉坦病毒肺综合征爆发与 2026 年 4 月至 5 月的游轮旅行有关。

2026年4月，一艘游轮上爆发了由安第斯病毒（ANDV）引起的汉坦病毒肺综合征（HPS）疫情。在一项跨国调查期间，获得了 13 个已识别病例中的 10 个 ANDV 基因组序列，该调查旨在共享初始原始数据，以便进行相互质量控制的联合分析。基因组数据、症状发作和合理的接触模式有力地支持了紧密相连的疫情集群，与登船前发生的单一或极少数人畜共患病溢出事件相一致，随后在船上进行了人际传播。这次疫情的爆发代表了一种已知在特定条件下能够在人类之间传播的病毒在国际上的转移，并不意味着出现了一种不寻常的病毒株。

Hantavirus pulmonary syndrome outbreak linked to cruise ship travel in April-May 2026.

In April 2026, a hantavirus pulmonary syndrome (HPS) outbreak caused by Andes virus (ANDV) occurred on a cruise ship. ANDV genome sequences were obtained for 10 of the 13 identified cases during a cross-country investigation established to share initial raw data for joint analysis with reciprocal quality control. The genomic data, symptom onsets, and plausible contact patterns strongly support a tightly linked outbreak cluster compatible with a single or very few zoonotic spillover events prior to embarkment followed by human-to-human transmission onboard. The outbreak represents the international displacement of a virus already known to be capable, under specific conditions, to transmit among humans, and does not suggest emergence of a unusual viral strain.

Long-term local circulation of an Andes virus lineage in northern Patagonia is linked to the M/V Hondius outbreak.

In 2026, a hantavirus pulmonary syndrome (HPS) outbreak caused by Andes virus (ANDV) occurred on a cruise ship. Analyses revealed an unexpected relationship to an outbreak in Argentina in 2018. Retrospective investigation clarified the 2018 outbreak could involve person-to-person transmission and a common ANDV lineage spanned HPS cases from 2018 to 2025 in a geographically defined area in northern Patagonia, without widespread community transmission of ANDV. Route reconstruction for the index cases in the cruise ship outbreak identified geographically plausible exposures to viruses of this lineage in Argentina, but their timing was inconsistent with the expected ANDV incubation period. The infection source for the cruise ship outbreak cannot be resolved with current information, highlighting the need to expand viral surveillance in the ANDV endemic region.

汉坦病毒的免疫原性及疫苗保护效果评价：疫苗株与中国部分省份代表性流行株的中和抗体比较研究。

由汉坦病毒（HV）引起的肾综合征出血热（HFRS）仍然是世界上重大的公共卫生负担。 20年前分离出的汉坦病毒（HTNV）和汉城病毒（SEOV）株的HFRS二价灭活疫苗（HFRSbiv）已在中国HFRS重点流行省份用于预防HFRS。经过几十年的综合防控措施的有效实施，我国HFRS已进入疫情低水平波动阶段。然而，病毒在啮齿动物宿主动物之间的持续传播可能导致病毒遗传多样性和免疫原性的动态变化，从而可能影响病毒的致病机制和疫苗的保护效果。有必要及时开展HV免疫原性监测和评估。通过筛选来自恢复期 HFRS 患者和接受 HFRSbiv 标准化免疫的个体的血清样本，建立了一组血清。使用基于酶联免疫吸附测定的微量中和试验（MN-ELISA）测量了针对 HTNV 疫苗株 84FLi、SEOV 疫苗株 L99 和来自不同基因型的三种代表性流行株（HTNV：SX26、SEOV：LN03 和 FJ-17）的中和抗体滴度。从所有选定的血清样本中均检测到 HV 中和抗体。 HFRS患者中，47.92%（23/48）被诊断为HTNV感染，27.08%（13/48）被诊断为SEOV感染，25%（12/48）未被分型。血清样本中检测到的针对疫苗株和流行株的中和抗体效价存在显着差异。对于 HTNV，患者和疫苗接种者血清针对疫苗株 84FLi 的几何平均滴度 (GMT) 均显着高于针对流行株 SX26 的几何平均滴度 (GMT)（1:386.08 vs. 1:223.04，𝑃 < 0.001；1:137.94 vs. 1:110.37，𝑃 < 0.05，分别）。对于SEOV，患者血清针对疫苗株L99的GMT显着低于针对流行株LN03的GMT（1:177.02 vs. 1:297.71，𝑃 < 0.01），并且接种者血清针对L99的GMT显着低于针对流行株FJ-17的GMT（1:97.52 vs. 1:243.71， 𝑃 < 0.001）。 SEOV 流行毒株 LN03 和 FJ-17 之间观察到显着的免疫原性异质性。引起HFRS的HV的遗传多样性和免疫原性具有一定的地理区域特征。不同基因型 HV 感染诱导的交叉中和抗体水平可能差异很大，但仍能提供足够的交叉保护。目前国内使用的HFRSbiv可诱导广泛的中和抗体，针对不同的流行毒株和基因型，交叉保护效果可能有所不同。加强地区特定流行病毒株遗传特征监测，定期评估疫苗保护效果与流行毒株的匹配程度，预测评估HV传播和致病风险，及时优化HFRS防控策略和措施具有重要意义。

Immunogenicity of Hantavirus and evaluation of vaccine protective efficacy: A comparative neutralizing antibody study of vaccine strains and representative epidemic strains from selected provinces in China.

Hemorrhagic fever with renal syndrome (HFRS) caused by Hantavirus (HV) remains a significant public health burden in the world. HFRS bivalent inactivated vaccine (HFRSbiv) with Hantaan virus (HTNV) and Seoul virus (SEOV) strains isolated 20 years ago have been used in key HFRS endemic provinces in China for HFRS prevention. After decades of effective implementation of comprehensive prevention and control measures, HFRS has entered a stage of epidemic fluctuation at low-level in China. However, the continuous transmission of the virus among rodent host animals may lead to dynamic changes in genetic diversity and immunogenicity of the virus, which may affect the pathogenic mechanism of the virus and the protective effect of vaccines. It is necessary to carry out timely monitoring and evaluation of the immunogenicity of HV. A panel of sera was established by screening serum samples from HFRS patients at convalescent phase and individuals who had received standardized immunization with HFRSbiv. Neutralizing antibody titers against HTNV vaccine strain 84FLi, SEOV vaccine strain L99, and three representative epidemic strains from different genotypes (HTNV: SX26, SEOV: LN03 and FJ-17) were measured using an enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay-based microneutralization test (MN-ELISA). HV-neutralizing antibodies were detected from all the selected serum samples. Among HFRS patients, 47.92% (23/48) were identified as HTNV infection, 27.08% (13/48) were with SEOV infection, and 25% (12/48) were untypable. There is a significant difference in the titers of neutralizing antibodies against vaccine strains and epidemic strains detected in the serum samples. For HTNV, both patient and vaccinee sera exhibited significantly higher geometric mean titer (GMT) against vaccine strain 84FLi than against epidemic strain SX26 (1:386.08 vs. 1:223.04, 𝑃 < 0.001; 1:137.94 vs. 1:110.37, 𝑃 < 0.05, respectively). For SEOV, patient sera showed significantly lower GMT against vaccine strain L99 than against epidemic strain LN03 (1:177.02 vs. 1:297.71, 𝑃 < 0.01), and vaccinee sera showed significantly lower GMT against L99 than against epidemic strain FJ-17 (1:97.52 vs. 1:243.71, 𝑃 < 0.001). Significant immunogenicity heterogeneity was observed between SEOV epidemic strains LN03 and FJ-17. The genetic diversity and immunogenicity of HV, which causes HFRS, have certain geographical regional characteristics. The levels of cross neutralizing antibodies induced by infection of HV from different genotypes may vary significantly, but still provide sufficient cross protection. The HFRSbiv currently used in China could induce broad neutralizing antibody, the cross-protective efficacy might vary against different circulating strains and genotypes. It is of great significance to strengthen the monitoring of genetic characteristics of regional specific circulating viral strains, regularly evaluate the matching degree between vaccine protection effectiveness and epidemic strains, predict and evaluate the transmission and pathogenic risks of HV, and timely optimize HFRS prevention and control strategies and measures.
