阿根廷东北部大西洋森林蝙蝠的狂犬病、冠状病毒和汉坦病毒监测。

人们越来越认识到蝙蝠是许多新出现和重新出现的人畜共患病原体的宿主。然而，人们对大多数宿主和病毒的分布以及每种蝙蝠在传播中的作用知之甚少，特别是在南美洲。这项研究筛查了正汉坦病毒、狂犬病和冠状病毒感染，并对 2022 年至 2025 年期间阿根廷米西奥内斯省的蝙蝠群落进行了特征分析。至少有 14 种蝙蝠被捕获 1214 次。通过 ELISA 检测血液样本中的正汉坦病毒特异性抗体。通过两种独立的实时 RT-PCR 检测对颊咽拭子进行筛选：泛狂犬病病毒检测和泛冠状病毒检测。在两个个体（Sturnira lilium 和 Artibeus lituratus）中检测到了狂犬病病毒 RNA，这是阿根廷在 Sturnira lilium 中首次确认的记录。在两种百合花、一种卡罗利亚百合花和一种斜纹草中检测到了冠状病毒 RNA。其中两个样本（对应于 C. perspicillata 和 A. lituratus）被成功鉴定为 Alphacoronavirus。未检测到针对正汉坦病毒的抗体。这些发现证实了食果蝙蝠中蝙蝠的高度多样性和病毒循环，支持综合监测。

Rabies, Coronavirus, and Hantavirus Surveillance in Bats From the Atlantic Forest of Northeastern Argentina.

Bats are increasingly recognized as reservoirs of numerous emerging and re-emerging zoonotic pathogens. However, little is known about the distribution of most hosts and viruses and the role of each bat species in transmission, particularly in South America. This study screened for orthohantavirus, rabies, and coronavirus infection, and characterized bat communities from Misiones Province, Argentina, during 2022-2025. At least 14 bat species were captured 1214 times. Blood samples were tested for orthohantavirus-specific antibodies by ELISA. Buccopharyngeal swabs were screened by two separate real-time RT-PCR assays: a pan-lyssavirus assay and a pan-coronavirus assay. Lyssavirus rabies RNA was detected in two individuals (Sturnira lilium and Artibeus lituratus), representing the first confirmed record in S. lilium in Argentina. Coronavirus RNA was detected in two Sturnira lilium, one Carollia perspicillata, and one Artibeus lituratus. Two of these samples, corresponding to C. perspicillata and A. lituratus, were successfully characterized as Alphacoronavirus. No antibodies against orthohantavirus were detected. These findings confirm high bat diversity and viral circulation in frugivorous bats, supporting integrated surveillance.

2023-2025年兰溪市啮齿动物人畜共患钩端螺旋体和汉坦病毒流行情况及遗传多样性。

钩端螺旋体病（由细菌钩端螺旋体引起）和汉坦病毒病（由病毒汉坦病毒引起）是重要的人畜共患病，啮齿类动物是主要宿主。在本研究中，我们调查了浙江省兰溪市农村啮齿类动物中钩端螺旋体和汉坦病毒的流行情况和遗传多样性。啮齿动物的钩端螺旋体携带率高于全国平均水平。所有汉坦病毒株均为首尔病毒（SEOV），与疫苗株表现出高度遗传差异。值得注意的是，鉴定了博格彼得森钩端螺旋体的一个独特的局部系统发育簇，表明潜在的疫苗不匹配和局部进化分歧。样本采集时间为2023年4月至2025年8月，2023-2024年每年4月至8月进行集中捕获，2025年4月追加采样。通过无菌解剖收集肝脏和肾脏组织。采用实时荧光PCR检测病原核酸，对阳性样本进行基因测序和系统发育分析。共捕获啮齿类动物350只，其中褐家鼠（褐鼠）为优势种（80.00%）。钩端螺旋体总体携带率为7.14%（25/350），其中褐藻钩端螺旋体携带率最高（7.86%）。汉坦病毒总体携带率为1.43%（5/350），主要分布于褐家鼠和黄胸鼠（黄胸鼠）。未检测到两种病原体的双重感染。遗传分析鉴定出 12 株钩端螺旋体菌株为问号钩端螺旋体，2 株为 L. borgpetersenii；后者显示出国内外菌株的遗传差异，表明潜在的进化趋势。这两种汉坦病毒株均为SEOV，与2021年国产SEOV株密切相关，但与疫苗株（如80-39、Z37）的核苷酸同源性仅为86.08-86.81%，表明存在自然变异。兰溪农村啮齿类动物钩端螺旋体携带率较高，汉坦病毒以SEOV型为主。褐家鼠是这两种病原体的主要宿主。加强有针对性的监测和防控措施。自然遗传变异表明潜在的抗原漂移和疫苗匹配减少，这需要进一步的血清学验证。应关注病原菌遗传变异对防治工作的影响。

Prevalence and Genetic Diversity of Zoonotic Leptospira and Hantavirus in Rodents, Lanxi City, 2023-2025.

Leptospirosis (caused by Leptospira, a bacterium) and hantavirus disease (caused by hantavirus, a virus) are important zoonoses with rodents as main reservoirs. In this study, we investigated the prevalence and genetic diversity of Leptospira and hantavirus in rodents from rural Lanxi City, Zhejiang Province. Rodents showed a higher Leptospira carriage rate than the national average. All hantavirus isolates were Seoul virus (SEOV) and displayed high genetic divergence from vaccine strains. Notably, a unique local phylogenetic cluster of Leptospira borgpetersenii was identified, indicating potential vaccine mismatch and local evolutionary divergence. Samples were collected from April 2023 to August 2025, with intensive trapping from April to August annually in 2023-2024 and additional sampling in April 2025. Liver and kidney tissues were collected via aseptic dissection. Real-time fluorescent PCR was used to detect pathogen nucleic acids, and gene sequencing and phylogenetic analysis were performed on positive samples. A total of 350 rodents were captured, with Rattus norvegicus (brown rat) as the dominant species (80.00%). The overall Leptospira carriage rate was 7.14% (25/350), with the highest rate in R. norvegicus (7.86%). The overall hantavirus carriage rate was 1.43% (5/350), distributed mainly in R. norvegicus and Rattus flavipectus (yellow-breasted rat). No coinfection with both pathogens was detected. Genetic analysis identified 12 Leptospira strains as Leptospira interrogans and 2 as L. borgpetersenii; the latter showed genetic differences from domestic and foreign strains, suggesting a potential evolutionary trend. The two hantavirus strains were both SEOV, closely related to domestic SEOV strains from 2021 but with only 86.08-86.81% nucleotide homology to vaccine strains (e.g., 80-39, Z37), indicating natural variation. Rodents in rural Lanxi have a relatively high Leptospira carriage rate, and hantavirus is dominated by the SEOV type. R. norvegicus is the main host of both pathogens. Targeted monitoring and prevention/control measures should be strengthened. Natural genetic variation suggests potential antigenic drift and reduced vaccine matching, which requires further serological validation. Attention should be paid to the impact of pathogen genetic variation on control efforts.

Climate change as an amplifier of hantavirus risk in South Asia: a neglected nexus.

Hantavirus is seroepidemiologically established in South Asian human populations and rodent reservoirs, with anti-hantavirus antibodies confirmed in occupational risk groups in India, an independent association with chronic kidney disease demonstrated in Sri Lanka, and co-infection with leptospirosis identified in more than one-fifth of hospitalized leptospirosis patients. Despite this, the intersection of these findings with the region's intensifying monsoon floods, rapid urbanization, and climate-driven rodent ecology remains entirely unexamined. This letter highlights the neglected climate-hantavirus nexus, identifies structural vulnerabilities that amplify transmission risk in South Asia, and calls for integrated flood-response surveillance and One Health coordination across the region.

宿主杂交使得重配的汉坦病毒谱系出现。

个体之间遗传物质的交换是大多数生命分支进化和多样化的关键驱动力。分段病毒可以通过基因组片段的重配来交换遗传物质。重配产生的新病毒株可以具有更大的感染范围和更高的毒力，尽管重配病毒在自然界中除流感之外的适应性优势的具体例子仍然很少。我们在此研究了图拉正汉坦病毒 (TULV) 的进化历史和重配后果，该病毒位于其储存宿主普通田鼠 (Microtus arvalis) 进化谱系之间的混合区。在 58 个诱捕点和 127 只受感染的田鼠中，我们在亲代 TULV 进化枝接触处的 12.5 公里宽的区域中检测到了 27 个 TULV 重配体，类​​似于与宿主一致的病毒杂交区域。系统基因组分析揭示了三个独立的重配事件，但大多数宿主杂交区主要由具有重配 M 片段的单一菌株主导，该片段编码表面糖蛋白。我们检测到由五个残基组成的糖蛋白 N 末端区域的进化枝特异性变异，其中两个残基显示出正选择的证据。七种糖蛋白的计算机 3D 建模证实，该 N 末端区域对于每个 TULV 进化枝和主要重配体都具有独特且特定的结构，并且是 TULV 糖蛋白唯一的结构可变区域。我们的研究结果表明，接触区亲本 TULV 进化枝之间的重配导致了可能适应杂交宿主的越界病毒表型。这证明了杂交区对于出现具有新颖进化轨迹的新病毒株的潜力。

Host hybridization enabled the emergence of a reassorted hantavirus lineage.

The exchange of genetic material between individuals is a key driver of evolution and diversification across most branches of life. Segmented viruses can exchange genetic material through reassortment of genomic segments. New viral strains that emerge from reassortments can have greater infection ranges and higher virulence, although concrete examples of the adaptive advantages of reassortants in nature apart from influenza remain rare. We studied here the evolutionary history and consequences of reassortment in Tula orthohantavirus (TULV) in a hybrid zone between evolutionary lineages of its reservoir host, the common vole (Microtus arvalis). Across 58 trapping sites and 127 infected voles, we detected 27 TULV reassortants in a 12.5 km broad zone at the contact of the parental TULV clades, resembling a viral hybrid zone concordant with the hosts'. Phylogenomic analyses revealed three independent reassortment events, but most of the host hybrid zone was dominated by a single strain with a reassorted M-Segment, which encodes the surface glycoprotein. We detected clade-specific variation in the glycoprotein's N-terminal region consisting of five residues, two of which showed evidence of positive selection. In silico 3D modeling of seven glycoproteins confirmed that this N-terminal region has a unique and specific structure for each TULV clade and the dominant reassortants and is the only structurally variable region of the TULV glycoprotein. Our findings suggest that reassortment between the parental TULV clades in the contact region has resulted in a transgressive virus phenotype potentially adapted to hybrid hosts. This demonstrates the potential of zones of hybridization for the emergence of new virus strains with novel evolutionary trajectories.
