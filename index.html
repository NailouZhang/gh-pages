2026 年安第斯病毒爆发的发病率、发病机制、临床表现和药理学管理。

汉坦病毒被认为是一种致命的人畜共患病原体，通常可以从啮齿动物传播给人类。然而，安第斯病毒（ANDV）是新世界汉坦病毒的一种，也可以在人与人之间传播。这是2026年游轮上汉坦病毒爆发的原因。汉坦病毒可迅速发展为严重的呼吸窘迫，称为汉坦病毒心肺综合征（HCPS）。由于汉坦病毒感染没有特定的治疗方法或批准的疫苗，因此早期诊断和重症监护支持至关重要。在HCPS病例中，根据感染的严重程度，及时给予退热药、升压药、液体治疗、心肺支持、体外膜肺氧合（ECMO）、机械通气和肾脏替代治疗（RRT）非常重要。目前，美国或欧洲还没有批准的疫苗。然而，各种疫苗技术，包括灭活疫苗、DNA疫苗、mRNA疫苗、病毒载体疫苗和蛋白质疫苗，均处于临床前和临床阶段。预防措施，包括患者隔离和身体距离，对于避免广泛传播至关重要。

Incidence, pathogenesis, clinical manifestations, and pharmacological management of the 2026 Andes virus outbreak.

Hantavirus is considered as a fatal zoonotic pathogen that can be commonly transmitted from rodents to humans. However, the Andes virus (ANDV), a type of New World hantavirus, can also be transmitted from human to human. It is the cause of the 2026 hantavirus outbreak on a cruise ship. Hantavirus can rapidly progress to a severe respiratory distress, known as hantavirus cardiopulmonary syndrome (HCPS). Since there is no specific treatment or approved vaccine for hantavirus infection, early diagnosis and critical care support are essential. In cases of HCPS, depending on the severity of the infection, it is important to administer antipyretics, vasopressors, fluid therapy, cardiopulmonary support, extracorporeal membrane oxygenation (ECMO), mechanical ventilation, and renal replacement therapy (RRT) in a timely manner. Currently, there is no approved vaccine in the United States or Europe. However, various vaccine technologies, including inactivated, DNA, mRNA, viral vector and protein-based vaccines, are in the preclinical and clinical phases. Preventive actions, including patient isolation and physical distancing, are crucial to avoiding widespread transmission.

安第斯病毒：从传播动力学到大流行的可能性。

2026 年 4 月 MV Hondius 游轮上多国爆发后，安第斯病毒 (ANDV) 最近成为一个重大公共卫生问题，乘客和船员中有 13 例确诊病例，3 人死亡。我们查阅了 PubMed 和 Google Scholar 数据库中的现有文献，并检索了包括世界卫生组织和国家公共卫生当局在内的国际卫生机构，以获取当前的疫情信息和最新的流行病学数据。 ANDV 是一种人畜共患正汉坦病毒，在智利南部和阿根廷流行，以长尾寡鼠为主要宿主。美洲每年大约发生 300 例汉坦病毒心肺综合征病例。该病毒通过内皮功能障碍和血管渗漏引起严重疾病，导致迅速进展的肺水肿和休克。人际传播是 ANDV 的特征，在长期密切接触过程中通过呼吸道和唾液途径发生。临床表现包括非特异性前驱症状，随后出现心肺快速恶化。诊断依赖于血清学检测和 RT-qPCR。治疗主要是支持性的，强调血流动力学监测，并在严重病例中及早考虑体外膜肺氧合。尽管 ANDV 很严重且死亡率很高，但其流行潜力似乎很低。然而，最近的疫情爆发凸显了国际监测协调和快速诊断能力对新出现的人畜共患感染的重要性。

Andes Virus: From Transmission Dynamics to Pandemic Potential.

Andes virus (ANDV) has recently emerged as a significant public health concern following a multi-country outbreak aboard the MV Hondius cruise ship in April 2026, with 13 identified cases and three deaths among passengers and crew. We reviewed available literature from PubMed and Google Scholar databases and searched international health agencies, including the World Health Organisation and national public health authorities, for current outbreak information and up-to-date epidemiological data. ANDV is a zoonotic orthohantavirus endemic in southern Chile and Argentina, with Oligoryzomys longicaudatus as its principal reservoir. Approximately 300 cases of hantavirus cardiopulmonary syndrome occur annually in the Americas. The virus causes severe disease through endothelial dysfunction and vascular leakage, resulting in rapidly progressive pulmonary edema and shock. Human-to-human transmission is characteristic of ANDV and occurs through respiratory and salivary routes during prolonged close contact. Clinical presentation includes nonspecific prodromal symptoms followed by rapid cardiopulmonary deterioration. Diagnosis relies on serological assays and RT-qPCR. Management is predominantly supportive, emphasising hemodynamic monitoring and early consideration of extracorporeal membrane oxygenation in severe cases. Despite ANDV's severity and high mortality, its epidemic potential seems to be low. However, the recent outbreak underscores the importance of international surveillance coordination and rapid diagnostic capabilities for emerging zoonotic infections.

安第斯病毒相关汉坦病毒心肺综合征的重症监护负担和结果的时间趋势：智利全国队列。

安第斯病毒引起汉坦病毒心肺综合征（HCPS），这是一种在智利和阿根廷中部和南部地区发现的严重疾病。最近，一艘游轮上报告了 HCPS 病例爆发，因此需要有关器官支持和结果的最新数据。本研究的目的是评估 HCPS 入院患者的器官支持使用情况和患者预后。我们使用诊断相关组（DGR）数据库分析了2019年1月1日至2024年12月31日期间在智利31家医院收治的患者。他们根据接受的最大呼吸支持进行分层：无呼吸支持、无创通气（NIV）、有创机械通气（MV）或体外膜肺氧合（ECMO）。住院时间（LOS）和院内死亡率是评估的主要结果。按 COVID-19 大流行前、大流行和大流行后时期进一步分析了院内死亡率。总共确定了 215 名 HCPS 患者，其中 69.3% 为男性，87% 为成人。在 148 名需要呼吸支持的患者中，14.2% 接受 NIV，36.5% 接受 MV，49.3% 需要 ECMO。住院时间为 8 [3-15] 天，院内死亡率为 20.5%。接受 MV 的患者病死率为 29.6%，接受 ECMO 的患者病死率为 32.9%。大流行期间与较高的院内死亡率独立相关（aOR 2.73 [1.06-7.63]；p = 0.045）。安第斯病毒引起的 HCPS 仍然与院内高死亡率和大量呼吸支持（尤其是 MV 和 ECMO）相关。此外，COVID-19 大流行与 HCPS 死亡率的显着增加有关。

Temporal trends in critical care burden and outcomes of Andes virus-associated hantavirus cardiopulmonary syndrome: a national Chilean cohort.

Andes virus causes hantavirus cardiopulmonary syndrome (HCPS), a severe disease found in central and southern parts of Chile and Argentina. Recently a case-outbreak of HCPS was reported on a cruise ship, therefore contemporary data on organ support and outcomes is required. The objective of this study was to assess organ support use and patient outcomes in patients admitted with HCPS. We used the Diagnosis-Related Groups (DGR) database to analyse patients admitted in 31 Chilean hospitals between January 1, 2019, and December 31, 2024. They were stratified by the maximal respiratory support received: no respiratory support, non-invasive ventilation (NIV), invasive mechanical ventilation (MV) or extracorporeal membrane oxygenation (ECMO). Hospital length of stay (LOS) and in-hospital mortality were the main outcomes assessed. In-hospital mortality was further analysed by pre-COVID-19 pandemic, pandemic, and post-pandemic periods. A total of 215 patients with HCPS were identified, of whom 69.3% were males and 87% were adults. Of 148 patients who required respiratory support, 14.2% received NIV, 36.5% received MV and 49.3% required ECMO. Hospital LOS was 8 [3-15] days and in-hospital mortality was 20.5%. Case fatality rates were 29.6% for patients receiving MV and 32.9% for those receiving ECMO. The pandemic period was independently associated with higher in-hospital mortality (aOR 2.73 [1.06-7.63]; p = 0.045). HCPS caused by Andes virus remains associated with high in-hospital mortality and substantial amounts of respiratory support, particularly MV and ECMO. Moreover, the COVID-19 pandemic was associated with a significant increase in HCPS mortality.
