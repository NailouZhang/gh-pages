医护人员的安第斯病毒暴露和医院传播事件：系统评价。

安第斯病毒（ANDV）是一种后果严重的传染病，死亡率很高。 2026 年 5 月 2 日，世界卫生组织报告了多国 ANDV 疫情，引发了有关向医护人员 (HCP) 传播风险的问题。我们根据 PRISMA 指南 (PROSPERO:CRD420261283806) 对描述与医疗保健相关的 ANDV 暴露或 HCP 传播事件的研究进行了系统评价。其中包括报告 7 个事件的 8 项研究，总共描述了 17 个与医疗保健相关的案例。总体而言，共有 207 人暴露，其中包括 118 名医护人员，导致医护人员中 8 人感染、4 人死亡。描述 HCP 感染的研究报告没有使用或不充分使用个人防护装备 (PPE)。据报道，由于延迟实施基于传播的预防措施或违反感染控制实践，ANDV 被 HCP 传播。已发布的暴露事件的局限性凸显了对暴露事件和结果以及所实施的感染预防措施进行标准化报告的必要性。

Andes Virus Exposure and Nosocomial Transmission Events to Healthcare Personnel: A Systematic Review.

Andes virus (ANDV) is a high-consequence infectious disease with substantial mortality. On 2 May 2026, the World Health Organization reported a multinational ANDV outbreak, raising questions regarding the risk of transmission to healthcare personnel (HCP). We performed a systematic review per PRISMA guidelines (PROSPERO:CRD420261283806) for studies describing healthcare-associated ANDV exposure or transmission events to HCP. Eight studies reporting on 7 events were included, describing a total of 17 healthcare-associated cases. Overall, 207 individuals were exposed, including 118 HCP, resulting in 8 infections and 4 deaths among HCP. Studies describing HCP infections reported none or inadequate personal protective equipment (PPE) use. Transmission of ANDV to HCP has been reported in the setting of delayed implementation of transmission-based precautions or breaches in infection control practice. Limitations in published exposure events highlight the need for standardized reporting of exposure events and outcomes as well as infection prevention measures implemented.

通过汉坦病毒防范措施加强索马里对新发传染病的准备

摘要

新出现的传染病继续对全球公共卫生安全构成重大威胁，低收入和中等收入国家承受着不成比例的脆弱性负担。 2026 年 5 月，世界卫生组织 (WHO) 非洲区域办事处和非洲疾病控制与预防中心 (非洲 CDC) 召开了一次关于汉坦病毒防范的联合技术简报会（网络研讨会），强调了为整个非洲大陆的汉坦病毒和其他人畜共患威胁做好积极准备的重要性。虽然索马里没有报告汉坦病毒爆发，也没有人类汉坦病毒传播的直接证据，但这次通报提供了一个及时的机会，可以批判性地审查索马里更广泛地发现和应对新出现的传染病的能力。本评论根据世卫组织-非洲疾病预防控制中心简报中强调的关键准备领域（包括监测、实验室诊断、风险沟通和“同一个健康”协调）评估了索马里当前的公共卫生基础设施，并确定了需要紧急政策关注的关键差距。根据已发表的证据和国际框架，我们提供针对具体情况的建议，以在危机出现之前加强索马里的卫生安全架构。对于一个处于复杂人道主义状况和持续流行病风险交汇处的国家来说，积极投资做好准备既是道义上的责任，也是战略上的必要性。

Strengthening readiness for emerging infectious diseases in Somalia through hantavirus preparedness

Abstract

Emerging infectious diseases continue to pose substantial threats to global public health security, with low- and middle-income countries bearing a disproportionate burden of vulnerability. In May 2026, the World Health Organization (WHO) Regional Office for Africa and the Africa Centres for Disease Control and Prevention (Africa CDC) convened a joint technical briefing (webinar) on hantavirus preparedness, underscoring the importance of proactive readiness for this and other zoonotic threats across the African continent. While no hantavirus outbreak and no direct evidence of human hantavirus circulation have been reported in Somalia, this briefing presents a timely opportunity to critically examine Somalia’s capacity to detect and respond to emerging infectious diseases more broadly. This commentary evaluates Somalia’s current public health infrastructure against the key preparedness domains highlighted in the WHO–Africa CDC briefing including surveillance, laboratory diagnostics, risk communication, and One Health coordination and identifies critical gaps that require urgent policy attention. Drawing on published evidence and international frameworks, we offer context-specific recommendations to strengthen Somalia’s health security architecture before a crisis emerges. Proactive investment in preparedness is both a moral imperative and a strategic necessity for a country at the intersection of complex humanitarian conditions and persistent epidemic risk.

构建危机汉坦病毒 dalam Komunikasi Pemasaran Pariwisata

这项研究探讨了印度尼西亚的英语在线媒体如何描述涉及 MV Hondius 的汉坦病毒危机，以及这种框架如何与跨国健康危机期间的旅游营销传播相关。该研究利用定性内容分析和框架分析，分析了 2026 年 5 月 4 日至 31 日期间从《雅加达环球报》和《雅加达邮报》存档的 16 篇独特且相关的新闻文章，关键词为“MV Hondius”、“汉坦病毒”和“安第斯病毒”。该语料库是通过有目的的抽样、相关性筛选和重复数据删除从 27 篇初始出版物中选出的。这些文章根据出版阶段、主导框架、参与者可见性、风险重点和营销传播影响进行编码。调查结果显示，该新闻不仅报道了医疗事件，还构建了一种危机叙事，从疫情升级到跨境控制和声誉正常化。主导框架是跨国治理，其次是声誉恢复、旅游流动性和健康威胁。该研究认为，旅游危机信息应从生态系统危机沟通的角度将健康准确性、出行透明度、目的地保证和基于证据的声誉恢复结合起来。

Framing Krisis Hantavirus dalam Komunikasi Pemasaran Pariwisata

This study examines how English-language online media in Indonesia frame the hantavirus crisis involving MV Hondius and how such framing is relevant to tourism marketing communication during a transnational health crisis. Using qualitative content analysis and framing analysis, the study analyzes 16 unique and relevant news articles archived from Jakarta Globe and The Jakarta Post between 4 and 31 May 2026 with the keywords “MV Hondius”, “hantavirus”, and “Andes virus”. The corpus was selected from 27 initial publications through purposive sampling, relevance screening, and deduplication. The articles were coded according to publication phase, dominant frame, actor visibility, risk emphasis, and marketing communication implications. The findings show that the news did not merely report a medical event but constructed a crisis narrative that moved from outbreak escalation to cross-border control and reputational normalization. The dominant frame was cross-national governance, followed by reputational recovery, tourism mobility, and health threat. The study argues that tourism crisis messages should combine health accuracy, mobility transparency, destination reassurance, and evidence-based reputation recovery within an eco-systemic crisis communication perspective.
