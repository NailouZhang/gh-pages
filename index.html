安第斯病毒疫苗衍生的人抗体对叙利亚仓鼠的保护作用评估：使用一期试验血清的被动转移研究。

安第斯病毒 (ANDV) 会导致严重的汉坦病毒肺部综合征，并且具有独特的人际传播能力，目前尚无获得许可的疫苗或治疗方法。一期试验 (NCT03682107) 表明，针对 Gn/Gc 包膜蛋白的 ANDV DNA 疫苗可在人体中引发中和抗体。我们评估了这些人类抗体是否可以在动物模型中预防致命疾病。在两次独立实验中，在致死性 ANDV 攻击前一天，将来自已接种疫苗的人类受试者的第 197 天血清汇集、纯化并被动转移至雌性叙利亚仓鼠。对照包括正常人 IgG、PBS（阴性对照）和 SAB-400（阳性对照）。使用伪病毒粒子中和试验（PsVNA）和噬菌斑减少中和试验（PRNT）对中和抗体水平进行定量，以确定预测存活的探索性截止滴度。与正常 IgG 不同，来自 DNA 疫苗接种受试者的人类抗体在两项实验中都提供了针对致命性 ANDV 攻击的显着保护作用。根据分析群体和模型参数，预测保护作用的探索性截止滴度确定为 PsVNA50 的 31-184 和 PRNT50 的 20-80。在已建立的动物模型中，疫苗诱导的人类抗体足以防止致命的 ANDV 感染。如果得到证实，这些结果表明中和抗体滴度可以作为替代终点并与未来汉坦病毒 Gn/Gc 靶向疫苗的保护相关。国防健康计划和国立卫生研究院 (NIH)。

Evaluation of Andes virus vaccine-derived human antibody for protection in Syrian hamsters: a passive transfer study using phase 1 trial sera.

Andes virus (ANDV) causes severe hantavirus pulmonary syndrome and is uniquely capable of person-to-person transmission, with no licenced vaccines or therapeutics available. A Phase 1 trial (NCT03682107) demonstrated that an ANDV DNA vaccine targeting Gn/Gc envelope proteins elicits neutralising antibodies in humans. We evaluated whether these human antibodies protect against lethal disease in an animal model. Day 197 sera from vaccinated human subjects were pooled, purified, and passively transferred to female Syrian hamsters one day prior to lethal ANDV challenge across two independent experiments. Controls included normal human IgG, PBS (negative controls), and SAB-400 (positive control). Neutralising antibody levels were quantified using pseudovirion neutralisation assays (PsVNA) and plaque reduction neutralisation tests (PRNT) to determine exploratory cutoff titres predicting survival. Human antibodies from DNA-vaccinated subjects-unlike normal IgG-conferred significant protection against lethal ANDV challenge in both experiments. The exploratory cutoff titres predicting protection were determined to be 31-184 for PsVNA50 and 20-80 for PRNT50, depending on the analysis population and model parameters. Vaccine-induced human antibodies are sufficient to protect against lethal ANDV infection in an established animal model. If confirmed, these results indicate that neutralising antibody titres could serve as a surrogate endpoint and correlate of protection for future hantavirus Gn/Gc-targeted vaccines. Defense Health Program and the National Institutes of Health (NIH).

综合免疫信息学和分子动力学方法揭示了一种有前途的针对汉坦病毒的多表位疫苗。

汉坦病毒是一种啮齿类动物传播的人畜共患病原体，可引起肾综合征出血热 (HFRS) 和汉坦病毒肺综合征 (HPS)，在全球范围内具有很高的发病率和死亡率。目前还没有普遍批准的人类疫苗，因此安全、有效的疫苗成为公共卫生优先事项。采用免疫信息学策略设计针对汉坦正汉坦病毒糖蛋白前体（GP_HANTL）的多表位疫苗构建体（MEVC）。预测并筛选细胞毒性 T 淋巴细胞 (CTL)、辅助 T 淋巴细胞 (HTL) 和 B 细胞表位的抗原性、免疫原性、MHC 结合亲和力和群体覆盖率。选定的表位与 EAAAK、AAY、GPPGG 和 KK 连接子连接，并与 β-防御素佐剂融合。通过物理化学表征、结构建模、分子对接、分子动力学（MD）模拟、免疫模拟、密码子优化和计算机克隆对构建体进行评估。最终的构建体包含 10 个 CTL、10 个 HTL 和 5 个 B 细胞表位，全球人群覆盖率为 99.24%。结构验证显示 95% 的残基位于有利的 Ramachandran 区域，ERRAT 评分为 82。分子对接为选定的 MEVC-TLR4 复合物产生了有利的 ClusPro 评分 (- 1273.6)。免疫模拟预测了强烈的体液和细胞反应，包括抗体升高、记忆 B 细胞和 CTL/HTL 激活。密码子优化产生的 CAI 为 0.94，支持大肠杆菌的高效表达。 100 ns MD 模拟显示 MEVC 的显着构象适应，而 TLR4 保持相对稳定；伴随的 Rg 和 SASA 趋势表明 MEVC 在轨迹过程中采用了更紧凑的状态。所提出的 MEVC 表现出良好的预测免疫原性、良好的结构特征、广泛的人群覆盖度和良好的表达特征。这些发现支持实验验证和制定针对汉坦病毒感染的预防策略。在线版本包含 https://doi.org/10.1007/s40203-026-00782-6 上提供的补充材料。

Integrated immunoinformatics and molecular dynamics approaches reveal a promising multi-epitope vaccine against hantavirus.

Hantavirus is a rodent-borne zoonotic pathogen causing Hemorrhagic Fever with Renal Syndrome (HFRS) and Hantavirus Pulmonary Syndrome (HPS), with substantial global morbidity and mortality. No universally approved human vaccine exists, making a safe, effective vaccine a public health priority. An immunoinformatics strategy was used to design a multi-epitope vaccine construct (MEVC) targeting the glycoprotein precursor (GP_HANTL) of Hantaan orthohantavirus. Cytotoxic T lymphocyte (CTL), helper T lymphocyte (HTL), and B-cell epitopes were predicted and screened for antigenicity, immunogenicity, MHC-binding affinity, and population coverage. Selected epitopes were joined with EAAAK, AAY, GPGPG, and KK linkers and fused to a β-defensin adjuvant. The construct was evaluated by physicochemical characterization, structural modeling, molecular docking, molecular dynamics (MD) simulation, immune simulation, codon optimization, and in silico cloning. The final construct comprised 10 CTL, 10 HTL, and 5 B-cell epitopes, with 99.24% global population coverage. Structural validation showed 95% of residues in favored Ramachandran regions and an ERRAT score of 82. Molecular docking produced a favorable ClusPro score (- 1273.6) for the selected MEVC-TLR4 complex. Immune simulations predicted robust humoral and cellular responses, including elevated antibodies, memory B cells, and CTL/HTL activation. Codon optimization yielded a CAI of 0.94, supporting efficient Escherichia coli expression. A 100-ns MD simulation showed substantial conformational adaptation of the MEVC while TLR4 remained comparatively stable; accompanying Rg and SASA trends indicated that the MEVC adopted a more compact state during the trajectory. The proposed MEVC demonstrated favorable predicted immunogenicity, favorable structural characteristics, broad population coverage, and favorable expression characteristics. These findings support experimental validation and development of a preventive strategy against Hantavirus infection. The online version contains supplementary material available at https://doi.org/10.1007/s40203-026-00782-6.

HFRS 的表观遗传学：将免疫、发病机制和疫苗诱导的保护联系起来。

肾综合征出血热 (HFRS) 由旧世界汉坦病毒引起，包括汉坦病毒 (HTNV) 和首尔病毒 (SEOV)，是一个重要的公共卫生问题，特别是在亚洲。临床表现包括发热性疾病、血管渗漏、血小板减少、出血和急性肾损伤。疾病严重程度变化以及疫苗诱导免疫的变异性和持久性的潜在机制仍不完全清楚。表观遗传机制，包括 DNA 甲基化、组蛋白修饰、染色质调节和非编码 RNA 介导的调节，可以影响多种生物环境中的抗病毒免疫和免疫细胞功能；然而，它们对 HFRS 的具体贡献仍未得到充分表征。这篇结构化叙述性综述检查了汉坦病毒特异性证据以及其他病毒感染和疫苗接种环境的相关发现，重点关注炎症反应、内皮功能障碍、调节性非编码 RNA、训练有素的免疫和疫苗学中的潜在应用。 HTNV 实验研究和调节 RNA 的临床研究已经确定了与汉坦病毒-宿主相互作用相关的候选途径；然而，这些发现并没有证实表观遗传机制决定 HFRS 的严重程度或临床结果。同样，临床前汉坦病毒疫苗研究和早期临床评估提供了有关免疫原性和安全性的证据，但没有证明保护性免疫的表观遗传介导。因此，主要与汉坦病毒心肺综合征 (HCPS) 相关的安第斯病毒 (ANDV) 和其他病毒系统的发现被认为是比较或背景证据，不能直接推广到 HFRS。这篇综述确定了关键的知识差距，并概述了纵向、细胞解析的表观基因组分析、功能验证和综合多组学如何阐明因果机制，解释疾病结果和疫苗反应的个体间差异，并确定表观遗传生物标志物和干预措施的潜在转化价值。

Epigenetics of HFRS: Linking Immunity, Pathogenesis, and Vaccine-Induced Protection.

Haemorrhagic fever with renal syndrome (HFRS), caused by Old World hantaviruses, including Hantaan virus (HTNV) and Seoul virus (SEOV), is an important public-health concern, particularly in Asia. Clinical manifestations range from febrile illness to vascular leakage, thrombocytopenia, haemorrhage and acute kidney injury. The mechanisms underlying variation in disease severity and the variability and durability of vaccine-induced immunity remain incompletely understood. Epigenetic mechanisms, including DNA methylation, histone modifications, chromatin regulation and non-coding RNA-mediated regulation, can influence antiviral immunity and immune-cell function in several biological settings; however, their specific contributions to HFRS remain insufficiently characterized. This structured narrative review examines hantavirus-specific evidence alongside relevant findings from other viral infections and vaccination settings, focusing on inflammatory responses, endothelial dysfunction, regulatory non-coding RNAs, trained immunity and potential applications in vaccinology. Experimental HTNV studies and clinical investigations of regulatory RNAs have identified candidate pathways associated with hantavirus-host interactions; however, these findings do not establish that epigenetic mechanisms determine HFRS severity or clinical outcomes. Similarly, preclinical hantavirus vaccine studies and early clinical evaluations provide evidence regarding immunogenicity and safety but do not demonstrate epigenetic mediation of protective immunity. Findings from Andes virus (ANDV), associated primarily with hantavirus cardiopulmonary syndrome (HCPS), and other viral systems are therefore considered comparative or contextual evidence, not directly generalizable to HFRS. This review identifies critical knowledge gaps and outlines how longitudinal, cell-resolved epigenomic profiling, functional validation and integrated multi-omics could clarify causal mechanisms, explain interindividual variation in disease outcomes and vaccine responses, and establish the potential translational value of epigenetic biomarkers and interventions.

游轮上的汉坦病毒：COVID-19 教给我们的。

在 2019 年冠状病毒病 (COVID-19) 大流行之初，人们对气溶胶传播机制（严重急性呼吸综合征冠状病毒 2 (SARS-CoV-2) 的主要传播方式）的认识被延迟，导致有关非药物干预措施的早期建议不足，以及关于如何降低感染风险的初步信息传递不佳。我们强调了安第斯病毒（ANDV）在 MV Hondius 游轮上传播的关键流行病学细节，以及之前报告的阿根廷境内已确认的人传人传播的疫情期间的关键流行病学细节，这表明气溶胶传播可能是在允许的环境条件下源病例和易感接触者之间 ANDV 传播的关键机制。

Hantavirus on a Cruise Ship: What COVID-19 Taught Us.

Delayed recognition of the mechanics of aerosol transmission-the primary mode of spread of severe acute respiratory syndrome coronavirus 2 (SARS-CoV-2)-at the beginning of the coronavirus disease 2019 (COVID-19) pandemic led to inadequate early recommendations regarding nonpharmaceutical interventions and poor initial messaging about how to reduce infection risk. We highlight key epidemiological details of Andes virus (ANDV) transmission on the MV Hondius cruise ship and during prior reported outbreaks with confirmed person-to-person spread in Argentina, which, together, suggest aerosol spread may be a key mechanism of transmission of ANDV between source cases and susceptible contacts in permissible environmental settings.
